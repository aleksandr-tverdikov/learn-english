Core verbs: technology#

The everyday regular verbs of technology — all four forms, pronunciation, Russian, and three examples that use more than one form.

← The grammar of verbs · All groups

Groups 1–22 cover the verbs that misbehave: irregular forms, phrasal verbs, and verb + preposition patterns. This tier covers the regular verbs, which were never a category here — which is why a dictionary built entirely of irregular and phrasal verbs had no work, no need, no ask and no explain.

Regular does not mean effortless. The forms are still where learners fail: carry → carried, stop → stopped, agree → agreed. Every entry spells all four out, and a Contrast line appears wherever the spelling or a required preposition is the trap.

open#

Pronunciation
/ˈoʊpən/ · OH-puhn
Русский
открывать, открыть; запускать, запустить (программу); открывать, открыть (дверь, окно, коробку); открываться (о магазине), начинать работу; открывать (заведение); открывать (счёт), заводить (дело), начинать (расследование)
Forms
open · opened · opened
Verb class
regular
Third person
opens · -ing form: opening
Transitivity
both — transitive (open the app), intransitive (the file opens slowly)
Contrast
No doubled n: opened, opening. The stress is on the first syllable, so the consonant stays single.

Open has 4 distinct senses.

1. To make a file, app, or window appear on a screen so you can use it. (открывать, открыть; запускать, запустить (программу))

  1. Open the app and sign in with your email.
  2. I opened the attachment and my laptop froze.
  3. The file is opening - give it a second.

2. To move a door, lid, or container so that it is no longer closed. (открывать, открыть (дверь, окно, коробку))

  1. Open the window; it is hot in here.
  2. She opened the letter with a kitchen knife.
  3. The door had been opened from the inside.

3. To begin business for the day, or to become available to the public. (открываться (о магазине), начинать работу; открывать (заведение))

  1. The bakery opens at six in the morning.
  2. They opened a second store downtown last spring.
  3. A new clinic is opening on Main Street.

4. To start something official, such as an account or an investigation. (открывать (счёт), заводить (дело), начинать (расследование))

  1. I want to open a savings account.
  2. Police opened an investigation into the fire.
  3. The committee has opened hearings on the crash.
Phrasal verbs
open up · open up · open up · open up · open up

close#

Pronunciation
/kloʊz/ · KLOHZ
Русский
закрывать, закрыть; закрываться, закрыться (о магазине, предприятии); заключать, заключить (сделку); закрывать сделку
Forms
close · closed · closed
Verb class
regular
Third person
closes · -ing form: closing
Transitivity
both — transitive (close the window), intransitive (the app closed by itself)
Contrast
The verb ends in a z sound (KLOHZ). The adjective close, meaning near, ends in an s sound (KLOHS).

Close has 3 distinct senses.

1. To make a file, window, or program stop being open. (закрывать, закрыть)

  1. Close the other tabs to speed up the browser.
  2. He closed the laptop without saving the file.
  3. The app keeps closing on its own.

2. Of a store or a business, to stop serving customers for the day, or to shut down for good. (закрываться, закрыться (о магазине, предприятии))

  1. The pharmacy closes at nine.
  2. The factory closed in 2008 and never reopened.
  3. Three branches have closed since January.

3. To finish and settle a sale or a business deal. (заключать, заключить (сделку); закрывать сделку)

  1. A good agent can close almost any sale.
  2. We closed on the house in June.
  3. The deal was closed late Friday afternoon.

close down · close in · close off · close out · close up · close up

Transitivity

Extract has 3 distinct senses.

1. To pull files or data out of an archive or a larger set. (извлекать, извлечь; распаковывать, распаковать)

  1. Extract the folder to your desktop.

  1. The dentist had to extract two teeth.

3. To get information, money, or a promise out of someone who does not want to give it. (выпытывать, выуживать; вырывать, вырвать (признание))

  1. Detectives spent all night trying to extract a confession.

Prompt has 3 distinct senses.

1. To ask a user on screen to do something, or to give a system an instruction. (предлагать (ввести), запрашивать; подсказывать, подсказать)

  1. The site will prompt you for a code.
  2. It prompted me to change my password.

  1. The letter prompted him to call his sister after ten years.
  2. One bad review can prompt a restaurant to change its whole menu.
  3. The crash has prompted calls for tighter rules.

3. To remind a performer or a speaker of words they have forgotten. (подсказывать, подсказать (текст, реплику))

  1. Someone offstage prompted her when she froze.
  2. The teacher had to prompt him twice during the recital.
  3. He got through the whole speech without being prompted once.
