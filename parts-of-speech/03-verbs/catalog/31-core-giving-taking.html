Core verbs: giving and taking#

The everyday regular verbs of giving and taking — all four forms, pronunciation, Russian, and three examples that use more than one form.

← The grammar of verbs · All groups

Groups 1–22 cover the verbs that misbehave: irregular forms, phrasal verbs, and verb + preposition patterns. This tier covers the regular verbs, which were never a category here — which is why a dictionary built entirely of irregular and phrasal verbs had no work, no need, no ask and no explain.

Regular does not mean effortless. The forms are still where learners fail: carry → carried, stop → stopped, agree → agreed. Every entry spells all four out, and a Contrast line appears wherever the spelling or a required preposition is the trap.

provide#

Pronunciation
/prəˈvaɪd/ · pruh-VYD
Русский
обеспечивать, предоставлять, предоставить
Forms
provide · provided · provided
Verb class
regular
Third person
provides · -ing form: providing
Transitivity
both — transitive (provide lunch), intransitive (provide for a family)
Contrast
Two patterns, both with a preposition: provide someone with something, or provide something for someone. Never provide me a pen.

To give someone what they need or ask for.

  1. The school provides free lunch to every student.
  2. They provided blankets after the storm.
  3. We are providing transportation for the out-of-town guests.
Phrasal verbs

contribute#

Pronunciation
/kənˈtrɪbjuːt/ · kuhn-TRIB-yoot
Русский
вносить вклад, жертвовать, внести; способствовать, быть одной из причин
Forms
contribute · contributed · contributed
Verb class
regular
Third person
contributes · -ing form: contributing
Transitivity
both — transitive (contribute an article), intransitive (everyone contributed)
Contrast
Stress is on the second syllable — kuhn-TRIB-yoot, not CON-tri-byoot — and the money goes to something: contribute to a fund.

Contribute has 2 distinct senses.

1. To give money, effort, or ideas as your part of something shared. (вносить вклад, жертвовать, внести)

  1. Everyone at work contributes five dollars a month.
  2. She contributed two hundred dollars to the relief fund.
  3. He has contributed three articles to the magazine this year.

2. To be one of the causes of something. (способствовать, быть одной из причин)

  1. Smoking contributes to heart disease.
  2. Poor lighting contributed to the accident.
  3. Several factors have contributed to the delay.
Phrasal verbs

/poʊst/ · POHST
Русский
публиковать, размещать, выложить; назначать (на должность, в место службы), направлять; выставлять (охрану); объявлять (финансовые результаты), сообщать; показать (прибыль, убыток); вносить залог, внести залог

Post has 4 distinct senses.

1. To put a notice, message, or picture where others can see it, especially online. (публиковать, размещать, выложить)

  1. They post the schedule every Sunday night.
  2. She posted the photos about an hour ago.
  3. He has been posting about the sale all week.

2. To send someone to a particular place to work or to stand guard. (назначать (на должность, в место службы), направлять; выставлять (охрану))

  1. The Army posted him to Germany for three years.
  2. They post a guard at the gate every night.
  3. She has been posted overseas twice since 2019.

3. To officially announce a financial result such as a profit or a loss. (объявлять (финансовые результаты), сообщать; показать (прибыль, убыток))

  1. The airline posted a record profit last quarter.
  2. Analysts expect the company to post another loss.
  3. The chain has posted three straight quarters of growth.

4. To pay money to a court so that an accused person can stay out of jail until the trial. (вносить залог, внести залог)

  1. His brother posted bail the same night.
  2. She could not afford to post a $10,000 bond.
  3. Bail was posted by an anonymous donor.
