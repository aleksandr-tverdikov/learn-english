# More verbs K-P

> Further verbs K-P — what a coverage check against two large verb lists showed was still missing.

[← The grammar of verbs](../README.md) &middot; [All groups](README.md)

Gathered by checking the catalog against roughly 21,000 scraped verb lemmas and writing entries for what was genuinely absent.

Most of that list was not worth writing — archaic verbs no living American uses, British-only spellings, narrow technical coinages, and a fair number of words that were not verbs at all — so these are the survivors of a deliberate rejection pass that threw out roughly nine of every ten candidates.

### kickstart

**Pronunciation:** /ˈkɪkstɑːrt/ &middot; *KIK-stahrt*
**Русский:** дать толчок, запустить; стимулировать
**Forms:** *kickstart* &middot; *kickstarted* &middot; *kickstarted*
**Third person:** *kickstarts* &middot; **-ing form:** *kickstarting*
**Verb class:** regular
**Transitivity:** transitive
**Contrast:** Often written with a hyphen: *kick-start*.

To make something begin or become active in a hurry.

1. A small grant can *kickstart* a business.
2. That one interview *kickstarted* her career.
3. The city is *kickstarting* the season with a free concert.
