Core verbs: weather and natural processes#

The everyday regular verbs of weather and natural processes — all four forms, pronunciation, Russian, and three examples that use more than one form.

← The grammar of verbs · All groups

Groups 1–22 cover the verbs that misbehave: irregular forms, phrasal verbs, and verb + preposition patterns. This tier covers the regular verbs, which were never a category here — which is why a dictionary of 1,291 verbs had no work, no need, no ask and no explain.

Regular does not mean effortless. The forms are still where learners fail: carry → carried, stop → stopped, agree → agreed. Every entry spells all four out, and a Contrast line appears wherever the spelling or a required preposition is the trap.

sleet#

Pronunciation
/sliːt/ · SLEET
Русский
идти (о дожде со снегом), сыпать ледяной крупой
Forms
sleet · sleeted · sleeted
Verb class
regular
Third person
sleets · -ing form: sleeting
Transitivity
intransitive
Contrast
Like rain and snow, it takes the empty subject it: It is sleeting, never The sky sleets.

To fall as a mix of rain and partly frozen snow.

  1. It usually sleets here for a day before it turns to real snow.
  2. It sleeted the whole way to Chicago.
  3. By noon it was sleeting hard and the roads were glazing over.
