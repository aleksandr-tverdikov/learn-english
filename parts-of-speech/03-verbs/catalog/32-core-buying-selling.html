Core verbs: money and trade#

The everyday regular verbs of money and trade — all four forms, pronunciation, Russian, and three examples that use more than one form.

← The grammar of verbs · All groups

Groups 1–22 cover the verbs that misbehave: irregular forms, phrasal verbs, and verb + preposition patterns. This tier covers the regular verbs, which were never a category here — which is why a dictionary of 1,291 verbs had no work, no need, no ask and no explain.

Regular does not mean effortless. The forms are still where learners fail: carry → carried, stop → stopped, agree → agreed. Every entry spells all four out, and a Contrast line appears wherever the spelling or a required preposition is the trap.

shop#

Pronunciation
/ʃɑːp/ · SHAHP
Русский
делать покупки, ходить по магазинам
Forms
shop · shopped · shopped
Third person
shops · -ing form: shopping
Transitivity
intransitive
Contrast
Double the p: shopped, shopping. You shop for what you are looking for.

To go to stores looking for things to buy.

  1. We usually shop on Saturday mornings.
  2. They shopped for a used car all weekend.
  3. I hate shopping for clothes.
