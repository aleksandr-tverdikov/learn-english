# Core verbs: giving and taking

> The everyday regular verbs of giving and taking — all four forms, pronunciation, Russian, and three examples that use more than one form.

[← The grammar of verbs](../README.md) &middot; [All groups](README.md)

Groups 1–22 cover the verbs that **misbehave**: irregular forms, phrasal verbs, and verb + preposition patterns. This tier covers the regular verbs, which were never a category here — which is why a dictionary of 1,291 verbs had no *work*, no *need*, no *ask* and no *explain*.

Regular does not mean effortless. The forms are still where learners fail: *carry → carried*, *stop → stopped*, *agree → agreed*. Every entry spells all four out, and a **Contrast** line appears wherever the spelling or a required preposition is the trap.

### provide

**Pronunciation:** /prəˈvaɪd/ &middot; *pruh-VYD*
**Русский:** обеспечивать, предоставлять, предоставить
**Forms:** *provide* &middot; *provided* &middot; *provided*
**Verb class:** regular
**Third person:** *provides* &middot; **-ing form:** *providing*
**Transitivity:** both — transitive (*provide lunch*), intransitive (*provide for a family*)
**Contrast:** Two patterns, both with a preposition: *provide* someone **with** something, or *provide* something **for** someone. Never *provide me a pen*.

To give someone what they need or ask for.

1. The school *provides* free lunch to every student.
2. They *provided* blankets after the storm.
3. We are *providing* transportation for the out-of-town guests.

**Forms:** *deduct* &middot; *deducted* &middot; *deducted*
**Verb class:** regular
**Third person:** *deducts* &middot; **-ing form:** *deducting*
**Transitivity:** transitive

To take an amount away from a total, especially from a payment.

1. They *deduct* taxes from every paycheck.
2. The hotel *deducted* the deposit from the bill.
3. Union dues are *deducted* automatically.

### save

**Pronunciation:** /seɪv/ &middot; *SAYV*
**Русский:** копить, откладывать, экономить
**Forms:** *save* &middot; *saved* &middot; *saved*
**Verb class:** regular
**Third person:** *saves* &middot; **-ing form:** *saving*
**Transitivity:** both — transitive (*save money*), intransitive (*they save for retirement*)

To keep money or things instead of spending or using them.

1. We *save* about three hundred dollars a month.
2. She *saved* for two years to buy it.
3. They have been *saving* every coupon in a drawer.

**Phrasal verbs:** [save from](22-verb-preposition.md#save-from)
