# Core verbs: giving and taking

> The everyday regular verbs of giving and taking — all four forms, pronunciation, Russian, and three examples that use more than one form.

[← The grammar of verbs](../README.md) &middot; [All groups](README.md)

Groups 1–22 cover the verbs that **misbehave**: irregular forms, phrasal verbs, and verb + preposition patterns. This tier covers the regular verbs, which were never a category here — which is why a dictionary built entirely of irregular and phrasal verbs had no *work*, no *need*, no *ask* and no *explain*.

Regular does not mean effortless. The forms are still where learners fail: *carry → carried*, *stop → stopped*, *agree → agreed*. Every entry spells all four out, and a **Contrast** line appears wherever the spelling or a required preposition is the trap.

### provide

**Pronunciation:** /prəˈvaɪd/ &middot; *pruh-VYD*
**Русский:** обеспечивать, предоставлять, предоставить
**Forms:** *provide* &middot; *provided* &middot; *provided*
**Verb class:** regular
**Third person:** *provides* &middot; **-ing form:** *providing*
**Transitivity:** both — transitive (*provide lunch*), intransitive (*provide for a family*)
**Contrast:** Two patterns, both with a preposition: *provide* someone **with** something, or *provide* something **for** someone. Never *provide me a pen*.

To give someone what they need or ask for.

1. The school *provides* free lunch to every student.
2. They *provided* blankets after the storm.
3. We are *providing* transportation for the out-of-town guests.

**Phrasal verbs:** [provide with, provide for](22-verb-preposition.md#provide-with-provide-for)

### contribute

**Pronunciation:** /kənˈtrɪbjuːt/ &middot; *kuhn-TRIB-yoot*
**Русский:** вносить вклад, жертвовать, внести; способствовать, быть одной из причин
**Forms:** *contribute* &middot; *contributed* &middot; *contributed*
**Verb class:** regular
**Third person:** *contributes* &middot; **-ing form:** *contributing*
**Transitivity:** both — transitive (*contribute an article*), intransitive (*everyone contributed*)
**Contrast:** Stress is on the second syllable — kuhn-TRIB-yoot, not CON-tri-byoot — and the money goes **to** something: *contribute to a fund*.

Contribute has 2 distinct senses.

**1. To give money, effort, or ideas as your part of something shared.** (вносить вклад, жертвовать, внести)

1. Everyone at work *contributes* five dollars a month.
2. She *contributed* two hundred dollars to the relief fund.
3. He has *contributed* three articles to the magazine this year.

**2. To be one of the causes of something.** (способствовать, быть одной из причин)

4. Smoking *contributes* to heart disease.
5. Poor lighting *contributed* to the accident.
6. Several factors have *contributed* to the delay.

**Phrasal verbs:** [contribute to](22-verb-preposition.md#contribute-to)

Share has 2 distinct senses.

4. Thanks for *sharing* your story with us.
5. She *shared* the news with her family first.
6. He never *shares* what he is feeling.
