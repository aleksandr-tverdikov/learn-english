# More verbs Q-S

> Further verbs Q-S — what a coverage check against two large verb lists showed was still missing.

[← The grammar of verbs](../README.md) &middot; [All groups](README.md)

Gathered by checking the catalog against roughly 21,000 scraped verb lemmas and writing entries for what was genuinely absent.

Most of that list was not worth writing — archaic verbs no living American uses, British-only spellings, narrow technical coinages, and a fair number of words that were not verbs at all — so these are the survivors of a deliberate rejection pass that threw out roughly nine of every ten candidates.

### quadruple

**Pronunciation:** /kwɑːˈdruːpəl/ &middot; *kwah-DROO-puhl*
**Русский:** увеличивать(ся) вчетверо, учетверять(ся)
**Forms:** *quadruple* &middot; *quadrupled* &middot; *quadrupled*
**Third person:** *quadruples* &middot; **-ing form:** *quadrupling*
**Verb class:** regular
**Transitivity:** both — transitive (*the city quadrupled its bus fleet*), intransitive (*prices quadrupled*)

To become four times as large, or to make something four times as large.

1. Prices could *quadruple* if the harvest fails.
2. The city *quadrupled* its bus fleet in a decade.
3. Enrollment has *quadrupled* since 2010.
