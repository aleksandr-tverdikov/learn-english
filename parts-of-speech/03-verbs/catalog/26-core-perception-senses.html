Core verbs: the senses#

The everyday regular verbs of the senses — all four forms, pronunciation, Russian, and three examples that use more than one form.

← The grammar of verbs · All groups

Groups 1–22 cover the verbs that misbehave: irregular forms, phrasal verbs, and verb + preposition patterns. This tier covers the regular verbs, which were never a category here — which is why a dictionary of 1,291 verbs had no work, no need, no ask and no explain.

Regular does not mean effortless. The forms are still where learners fail: carry → carried, stop → stopped, agree → agreed. Every entry spells all four out, and a Contrast line appears wherever the spelling or a required preposition is the trap.

watch#

Pronunciation
/wɑːtʃ/ · WAHCH
Русский
смотреть, наблюдать; посмотреть
Forms
watch · watched · watched
Third person
watches · -ing form: watching
Transitivity
both — transitive (watch a movie), intransitive (just stand and watch)
Contrast
Ends in -ch, so the third person takes -es: watches, never watchs.

To look at something for a period of time, following what happens.

  1. I watch the weather report before I leave for work.
  2. We watched the fireworks from the roof.
  3. He has been watching the same show for three hours.
