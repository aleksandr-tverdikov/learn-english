Core verbs: the senses#

The everyday regular verbs of the senses — all four forms, pronunciation, Russian, and three examples that use more than one form.

← The grammar of verbs · All groups

Groups 1–22 cover the verbs that misbehave: irregular forms, phrasal verbs, and verb + preposition patterns. This tier covers the regular verbs, which were never a category here — which is why a dictionary built entirely of irregular and phrasal verbs had no work, no need, no ask and no explain.

Regular does not mean effortless. The forms are still where learners fail: carry → carried, stop → stopped, agree → agreed. Every entry spells all four out, and a Contrast line appears wherever the spelling or a required preposition is the trap.

watch#

Pronunciation
/wɑːtʃ/ · WAHCH
Русский
смотреть, наблюдать; посмотреть; присматривать за, приглядеть за; следить за, остерегаться; быть осторожным с
Forms
watch · watched · watched
Verb class
regular
Third person
watches · -ing form: watching
Transitivity
both — transitive (watch a movie), intransitive (just stand and watch)
Contrast
Ends in -ch, so the third person takes -es: watches, never watchs.

Watch has 3 distinct senses.

1. To look at something for a period of time, following what happens. (смотреть, наблюдать; посмотреть)

  1. I watch the weather report before I leave for work.
  2. We watched the fireworks from the roof.
  3. He has been watching the same show for three hours.

2. To look after a person, an animal, or someone's things for a short time. (присматривать за, приглядеть за)

  1. Could you watch my bag for a second?
  2. Her mother watched the kids while she was at work.
  3. We have been watching their cat all week.

3. To be careful about something because it could cause trouble. (следить за, остерегаться; быть осторожным с)

  1. Watch your step — those boards are loose.
  2. He watched his language around the children.
  3. I'm watching my salt these days.
Phrasal verbs
watch out · watch out for · watch over

Survey has 3 distinct senses.

  1. A crew came out to survey the property line.
