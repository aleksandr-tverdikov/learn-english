Core verbs: farming and nature#

The everyday regular verbs of farming and nature — all four forms, pronunciation, Russian, and three examples that use more than one form.

← The grammar of verbs · All groups

Groups 1–22 cover the verbs that misbehave: irregular forms, phrasal verbs, and verb + preposition patterns. This tier covers the regular verbs, which were never a category here — which is why a dictionary built entirely of irregular and phrasal verbs had no work, no need, no ask and no explain.

Regular does not mean effortless. The forms are still where learners fail: carry → carried, stop → stopped, agree → agreed. Every entry spells all four out, and a Contrast line appears wherever the spelling or a required preposition is the trap.

plant#

Pronunciation
/plænt/ · PLANT
Русский
сажать, посадить; высаживать; подбрасывать, подбросить (улику); закладывать, заложить (бомбу); внедрять
Forms

Verb class
regular
Third person
plants · -ing form: planting
Transitivity
transitive

Plant has 2 distinct senses.

1. To put a seed or a young plant into the ground so that it will grow. (сажать, посадить; высаживать)

  1. We plant tomatoes every year around Mother's Day.
  2. They planted two hundred apple trees last spring.
  3. The corn was planted too early and the frost killed it.

2. To put something somewhere secretly so that it will be found or will do harm. (подбрасывать, подбросить (улику); закладывать, заложить (бомбу); внедрять)

  1. Someone planted drugs in his car.
  2. The bomb had been planted under a bench.
  3. They accused the agency of planting false stories in the press.
