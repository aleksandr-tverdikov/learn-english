Core verbs: work and business#

The everyday regular verbs of work and business — all four forms, pronunciation, Russian, and three examples that use more than one form.

← The grammar of verbs · All groups

Groups 1–22 cover the verbs that misbehave: irregular forms, phrasal verbs, and verb + preposition patterns. This tier covers the regular verbs, which were never a category here — which is why a dictionary of 1,291 verbs had no work, no need, no ask and no explain.

Regular does not mean effortless. The forms are still where learners fail: carry → carried, stop → stopped, agree → agreed. Every entry spells all four out, and a Contrast line appears wherever the spelling or a required preposition is the trap.

work#

Pronunciation
/wɜːrk/ · WURK
Русский
работать, поработать
Forms
work · worked · worked
Verb class
regular
Third person
works · -ing form: working
Transitivity
both — transitive (work long hours), intransitive (she works downtown)
Contrast
Work FOR a company, work AT a place, work ON a project, work WITH people. The preposition changes the meaning, not the verb.

To do a job, especially one you are paid for.

  1. I work for a small firm downtown.
  2. She worked late three nights in a row.
  3. He has been working here since 2019.
Phrasal verbs
work on, work for, work with
