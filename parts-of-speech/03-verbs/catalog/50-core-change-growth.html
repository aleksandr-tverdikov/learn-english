Core verbs: change and growth#

The everyday regular verbs of change and growth — all four forms, pronunciation, Russian, and three examples that use more than one form.

← The grammar of verbs · All groups

Groups 1–22 cover the verbs that misbehave: irregular forms, phrasal verbs, and verb + preposition patterns. This tier covers the regular verbs, which were never a category here — which is why a dictionary of 1,291 verbs had no work, no need, no ask and no explain.

Regular does not mean effortless. The forms are still where learners fail: carry → carried, stop → stopped, agree → agreed. Every entry spells all four out, and a Contrast line appears wherever the spelling or a required preposition is the trap.

transform#

Pronunciation
/trænsˈfɔːrm/ · trans-FORM
Русский
преобразовывать, преобразовать; превращать
Forms
transform · transformed · transformed
Verb class
regular
Third person
transforms · -ing form: transforming
Transitivity
both — transitive (transform a room), intransitive (the neighborhood transformed)
Contrast
Transform something into something else.

To change something completely, usually for the better.

  1. A coat of paint can transform a room.
  2. The company transformed itself in five years.
  3. The old factory is being transformed into apartments.
