# More verbs T-Z

> Further verbs T-Z — what a coverage check against two large verb lists showed was still missing.

[← The grammar of verbs](../README.md) &middot; [All groups](README.md)

Gathered by checking the catalog against roughly 21,000 scraped verb lemmas and writing entries for what was genuinely absent.

Most of that list was not worth writing — archaic verbs no living American uses, British-only spellings, narrow technical coinages, and a fair number of words that were not verbs at all — so these are the survivors of a deliberate rejection pass that threw out roughly nine of every ten candidates.

### tabulate

**Pronunciation:** /ˈtæbjəleɪt/ &middot; *TAB-yuh-layt*
**Русский:** сводить в таблицу, табулировать
**Forms:** *tabulate* &middot; *tabulated* &middot; *tabulated*
**Third person:** *tabulates* &middot; **-ing form:** *tabulating*
**Verb class:** regular
**Transitivity:** transitive

To arrange figures or facts in a table so that they can be compared.

1. Volunteers *tabulate* the results overnight.
2. The clerk *tabulated* the votes twice.
3. We are still *tabulating* the survey answers.

**Forms:** *yank* &middot; *yanked* &middot; *yanked*
**Third person:** *yanks* &middot; **-ing form:** *yanking*
**Verb class:** regular
**Transitivity:** both — transitive (*yank the cord*), intransitive (*yank on the rope*)

To pull something with one sudden, hard movement.

1. *Yank* the cord to start the mower.
2. He *yanked* the door open and ran.
3. She was *yanking* weeds out of the flower bed.
