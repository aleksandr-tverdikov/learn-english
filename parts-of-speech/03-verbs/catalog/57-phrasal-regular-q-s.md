# Phrasal verbs on regular bases: Q-S

> Phrasal verbs built on regular verbs — *check in*, *look up*, *figure out* — headwords Q-S.

[← The grammar of verbs](../README.md) &middot; [All groups](README.md)

The catalog's other 925 phrasal verbs are all built on **irregular** bases — *get*, *give*, *take*, *put*, *come*, *go* — because that is how the source material was organized. That left out the whole regular-base family, including some of the commonest phrasal verbs in English.

Each entry names its **base verb** and says whether it is **separable**, which is the hardest thing about phrasal verbs: *turn it off* and *turn off the light* are both fine, but *look after the kids* can never become ✗ *look the kids after*. A phrasal verb with several distinct meanings gets one entry per meaning, because they are different verbs.

### queue up

**Pronunciation:** /ˈkjuː ˌʌp/ &middot; *KYOO up*
**Русский:** выстраиваться в очередь, выстроиться в очередь; стоять в очереди
**Base verb:** *queue*
**Separable:** no object — *queue up at the door*; nothing ever stands between *queue* and *up*
**Forms:** *queue up* &middot; *queued up* &middot; *queued up*
**Third person:** *queues up* &middot; **-ing form:** *queuing up*
**Transitivity:** intransitive

To form a line and wait your turn.

1. Fans *queued up* overnight for tickets.
2. People were *queuing up* around the block by six.
3. You have to *queue up* at the counter to check in.

**Pronunciation:** /ˈsɪŋk ˌʌp/ &middot; *SINK up*
**Русский:** синхронизироваться; согласовывать, согласовать действия; созваниваться, созвониться
**Base verb:** *sync*
**Separable:** no object — the person you meet follows *with*: *sync up with the design team*
**Forms:** *sync up* &middot; *synced up* &middot; *synced up*
**Third person:** *syncs up* &middot; **-ing form:** *syncing up*
**Transitivity:** intransitive

To meet briefly so that everyone has the same information, or of two things, to match up.

1. Let's *sync up* Monday morning before the call.
2. We *synced up* with the design team yesterday.
3. The two calendars aren't *syncing up*.
