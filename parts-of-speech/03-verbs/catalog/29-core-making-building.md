# Core verbs: making and building

> The everyday regular verbs of making and building — all four forms, pronunciation, Russian, and three examples that use more than one form.

[← The grammar of verbs](../README.md) &middot; [All groups](README.md)

Groups 1–22 cover the verbs that **misbehave**: irregular forms, phrasal verbs, and verb + preposition patterns. This tier covers the regular verbs, which were never a category here — which is why a dictionary built entirely of irregular and phrasal verbs had no *work*, no *need*, no *ask* and no *explain*.

Regular does not mean effortless. The forms are still where learners fail: *carry → carried*, *stop → stopped*, *agree → agreed*. Every entry spells all four out, and a **Contrast** line appears wherever the spelling or a required preposition is the trap.

### create

**Pronunciation:** /kriˈeɪt/ &middot; *kree-AYT*
**Русский:** создавать, создать
**Forms:** *create* &middot; *created* &middot; *created*
**Verb class:** regular
**Third person:** *creates* &middot; **-ing form:** *creating*
**Transitivity:** transitive

To make something that did not exist before.

1. Let's *create* a shared folder for the photos.
2. She *created* the logo in a single afternoon.
3. The new rules have *created* a lot of confusion.

### form

**Pronunciation:** /fɔːrm/ &middot; *FORM*
**Русский:** формировать, образовывать, образовать; создавать, создать (организацию); основывать; собирать (группу); составлять, составить (мнение); формировать (привычку); складываться (о впечатлении)

**Third person:** *forms* &middot; **-ing form:** *forming*

Form has 3 distinct senses.

**1. To give something a shape, or to come into existence as a shape.** (формировать, образовывать, образовать)

1. *Form* the dough into a ball.

4. We plan to *form* a nonprofit next year.
5. The band *formed* in Austin in 2009.
6. A new government is being *formed* this week.

**3. To develop an idea, an opinion, or a habit in your mind.** (составлять, составить (мнение); формировать (привычку); складываться (о впечатлении))

7. Try not to *form* an opinion before you hear both sides.
