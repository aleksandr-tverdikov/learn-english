# Core verbs: work and business

> The everyday regular verbs of work and business — all four forms, pronunciation, Russian, and three examples that use more than one form.

[← The grammar of verbs](../README.md) &middot; [All groups](README.md)

Groups 1–22 cover the verbs that **misbehave**: irregular forms, phrasal verbs, and verb + preposition patterns. This tier covers the regular verbs, which were never a category here — which is why a dictionary built entirely of irregular and phrasal verbs had no *work*, no *need*, no *ask* and no *explain*.

Regular does not mean effortless. The forms are still where learners fail: *carry → carried*, *stop → stopped*, *agree → agreed*. Every entry spells all four out, and a **Contrast** line appears wherever the spelling or a required preposition is the trap.

### work

**Pronunciation:** /wɜːrk/ &middot; *WURK*
**Русский:** работать, поработать; работать, функционировать; действовать, подействовать, сработать; управлять (механизмом), уметь обращаться с, работать на (станке)
**Forms:** *work* &middot; *worked* &middot; *worked*
**Verb class:** regular
**Third person:** *works* &middot; **-ing form:** *working*
**Transitivity:** both — transitive (work long hours), intransitive (she works downtown)
**Contrast:** Work FOR a company, work AT a place, work ON a project, work WITH people. The preposition changes the meaning, not the verb.

Work has 3 distinct senses.

**1. To do a job, especially one you are paid for.** (работать, поработать)

1. I *work* for a small firm downtown.
2. She *worked* late three nights in a row.
3. He has been *working* here since 2019.

**2. For a machine, a system, or a plan to do what it is supposed to do.** (работать, функционировать; действовать, подействовать, сработать)

4. The elevator doesn't *work* again.
5. The medicine *worked* within an hour.
6. Their plan has *worked* better than anyone expected.

**3. To operate a machine or a piece of equipment.** (управлять (механизмом), уметь обращаться с, работать на (станке))

7. Can you *work* the projector?
8. She *worked* the register while the manager was out.
9. He has been *working* that crane for twenty years.

**Phrasal verbs:** [work around](58-phrasal-regular-t-z.md#work-around) &middot; [work in](58-phrasal-regular-t-z.md#work-in) &middot; [work off](58-phrasal-regular-t-z.md#work-off) &middot; [work on](58-phrasal-regular-t-z.md#work-on) &middot; [work on](58-phrasal-regular-t-z.md#work-on-1) &middot; [work on, work for, work with](22-verb-preposition.md#work-on-work-for-work-with) &middot; [work out](58-phrasal-regular-t-z.md#work-out) &middot; [work out](58-phrasal-regular-t-z.md#work-out-1) &middot; [work out](58-phrasal-regular-t-z.md#work-out-2) &middot; [work out](58-phrasal-regular-t-z.md#work-out-3) &middot; [work out to](58-phrasal-regular-t-z.md#work-out-to) &middot; [work over](58-phrasal-regular-t-z.md#work-over) &middot; [work through](58-phrasal-regular-t-z.md#work-through) &middot; [work up](58-phrasal-regular-t-z.md#work-up) &middot; [work up](58-phrasal-regular-t-z.md#work-up-1) &middot; [work up to](58-phrasal-regular-t-z.md#work-up-to)

### employ

**Pronunciation:** /ɪmˈplɔɪ/ &middot; *im-PLOY*
**Русский:** нанимать, нанять; держать на работе; применять, применить; использовать, употреблять
**Forms:** *employ* &middot; *employed* &middot; *employed*
**Verb class:** regular
**Third person:** *employs* &middot; **-ing form:** *employing*
**Transitivity:** transitive
**Contrast:** The -y follows a vowel, so nothing changes: employs, employed. Compare carry - carries - carried.

Employ has 2 distinct senses.

**1. To pay someone to work for you.** (нанимать, нанять; держать на работе)

1. The plant *employs* about two hundred people.
2. They *employed* him as a night manager.
3. She has been *employed* by the same firm for ten years.

**2. To use a method, a skill, or a tool for a purpose.** (применять, применить; использовать, употреблять)

4. The team *employs* several different testing methods.
5. Doctors *employed* a new technique to close the wound.
6. The film is *employing* the same trick for the third time.

### resign

**Pronunciation:** /rɪˈzaɪn/ &middot; *ri-ZYN*

Resign has 2 distinct senses.

**1. To officially leave a job or position.** (увольняться, уволиться; уходить в отставку)

4. She *resigned* herself to another year in the same job.
5. You have to *resign* yourself to the fact that he isn't coming back.
6. By then we were *resigned* to losing the deposit.

5. The officer *fired* two warning shots.
6. Missiles were *fired* from a ship offshore.

**3. To bake clay in a very hot oven so that it hardens.** (обжигать, обжечь (керамику))

7. We'll *fire* the kiln on Saturday.
8. She *fired* her first bowl last weekend.
9. These tiles are *fired* at 2,200 degrees.
