Core verbs: conflict#

The everyday regular verbs of conflict — all four forms, pronunciation, Russian, and three examples that use more than one form.

← The grammar of verbs · All groups

Groups 1–22 cover the verbs that misbehave: irregular forms, phrasal verbs, and verb + preposition patterns. This tier covers the regular verbs, which were never a category here — which is why a dictionary of 1,291 verbs had no work, no need, no ask and no explain.

Regular does not mean effortless. The forms are still where learners fail: carry → carried, stop → stopped, agree → agreed. Every entry spells all four out, and a Contrast line appears wherever the spelling or a required preposition is the trap.

attack#

Pronunciation
/əˈtæk/ · uh-TAK
Русский
нападать, напасть; атаковать
Forms
attack · attacked · attacked
Third person
attacks · -ing form: attacking
Transitivity
both — transitive (they attacked the village), intransitive (the enemy attacked at dawn)

To use violence against a person, place, or army in order to hurt them or take control.

  1. Two men attacked him outside the bar.
  2. A wounded animal will attack anyone who comes close.
  3. The base was attacked twice in the same week.
