# Core verbs: the senses

> The everyday regular verbs of the senses — all four forms, pronunciation, Russian, and three examples that use more than one form.

[← The grammar of verbs](../README.md) &middot; [All groups](README.md)

Groups 1–22 cover the verbs that **misbehave**: irregular forms, phrasal verbs, and verb + preposition patterns. This tier covers the regular verbs, which were never a category here — which is why a dictionary of 1,291 verbs had no *work*, no *need*, no *ask* and no *explain*.

Regular does not mean effortless. The forms are still where learners fail: *carry → carried*, *stop → stopped*, *agree → agreed*. Every entry spells all four out, and a **Contrast** line appears wherever the spelling or a required preposition is the trap.

### watch

**Pronunciation:** /wɑːtʃ/ &middot; *WAHCH*
**Русский:** смотреть, наблюдать; посмотреть
**Forms:** *watch* &middot; *watched* &middot; *watched*
**Third person:** *watches* &middot; **-ing form:** *watching*
**Transitivity:** both — transitive (watch a movie), intransitive (just stand and watch)
**Contrast:** Ends in -ch, so the third person takes -es: watches, never watchs.

To look at something for a period of time, following what happens.

1. I *watch* the weather report before I leave for work.
2. We *watched* the fireworks from the roof.
3. He has been *watching* the same show for three hours.

To look at something, especially deliberately or from a particular place.

### glance

**Pronunciation:** /ɡlæns/ &middot; *GLANS*
**Русский:** взглянуть, бросить взгляд
**Forms:** *glance* &middot; *glanced* &middot; *glanced*
**Third person:** *glances* &middot; **-ing form:** *glancing*
**Transitivity:** intransitive
**Contrast:** Almost always needs a preposition: glance at the menu, glance over a letter — not glance the menu.

To look at something very quickly and then look away.

1. I *glance* at my phone far too often.
2. She *glanced* over her shoulder and kept walking.
3. He was *glancing* at the clock every few seconds.

**Русский:** похлопывать, погладить рукой
**Forms:** *pat* &middot; *patted* &middot; *patted*
**Third person:** *pats* &middot; **-ing form:** *patting*
**Transitivity:** transitive
**Contrast:** The final t doubles: patted, patting.

To touch something lightly several times with a flat hand.

1. Coaches *pat* players on the back after a game.
2. He *patted* the seat next to him.
3. She was *patting* the dough into shape.
