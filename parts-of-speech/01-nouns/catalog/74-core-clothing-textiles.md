# Core vocabulary: fabric and textiles

> The everyday nouns of fabric and textiles — pronunciation, Russian, plural, and three examples each.

[← The grammar of nouns](../README.md) &middot; [All groups](README.md)

Part of the **core-vocabulary tier**. Groups 1–23 cover the nouns that misbehave; this tier covers the nouns you simply need to know. Entries are deliberately lean, because a regular countable noun has no grammatical story to tell. A **Contrast** line appears only where the word hides a genuine trap.

Where a word in this field *does* misbehave, it lives in the grammar tier instead and is not repeated here.

---
### textile

**Pronunciation:** /ˈtɛkstaɪl/ &middot; *TEK-styl*
**Русский:** текстиль, ткань
**Plural:** *textiles*
**Countability:** countable

A woven or knitted cloth, especially one made in a factory.

1. The town's economy ran on *textiles* for a hundred years.
2. She studies *textile* design at art school.
3. Cheap imported *textiles* put the old mill out of business.

### weave

**Pronunciation:** /wiːv/ &middot; *WEEV*
**Русский:** переплетение (нитей), плетение
**Plural:** *weaves*
**Countability:** countable

The way the threads of a fabric are crossed over each other.

1. This shirt has a tight *weave*, so the wind doesn't get through.
2. Hold it up to the light and you can see the *weave*.
3. Linen and cotton take very different *weaves*.

### knit

**Pronunciation:** /nɪt/ &middot; *NIT*
**Русский:** трикотаж, вязаное полотно
**Plural:** *knits*
**Countability:** countable
**Contrast:** The k is silent — *knit* sounds exactly like *nit*.

A fabric or garment made by looping yarn rather than weaving it.

1. In the fall she lives in soft *knits* and jeans.
2. That sweater is a loose *knit*, so it stretches.
3. The store keeps the *knits* on the back wall.

### satin

**Pronunciation:** /ˈsætən/ &middot; *SAT-uhn*
**Русский:** атлас (гладкая блестящая ткань)
**Countability:** uncountable
**Contrast:** Not the Russian сатин — English *satin* is атлас; сатин is *sateen*.

A smooth fabric that is shiny on one side and dull on the other.

1. Her wedding dress was ivory *satin*.
2. The box was lined with black *satin*.
3. *Satin* slips off the hanger if you're not careful.

### sateen

**Pronunciation:** /sæˈtiːn/ &middot; *sa-TEEN*
**Русский:** сатин (хлопчатобумажная ткань)
**Countability:** uncountable
**Contrast:** Stress on the second syllable, sa-TEEN, unlike *satin* (SAT-uhn).

A cotton fabric woven to give a soft sheen like satin.

1. These sheets are cotton *sateen*.
2. *Sateen* feels cool against the skin.
3. She picked a *sateen* lining for the coat.

### corduroy
