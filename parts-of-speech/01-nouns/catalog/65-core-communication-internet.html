Core vocabulary: communication and the internet#

The everyday nouns of communication and the internet — pronunciation, Russian, plural, and three examples each.

← The grammar of nouns · All groups

Part of the core-vocabulary tier. Groups 1–23 cover the nouns that misbehave; this tier covers the nouns you simply need to know. Entries are deliberately lean, because a regular countable noun has no grammatical story to tell. A Contrast line appears only where the word hides a genuine trap.

Where a word in this field does misbehave, it lives in the grammar tier instead and is not repeated here.

Contrast

  1. Add me as a contact so I don't go to spam.
  2. I lost all my contacts when the phone died.

  1. Do not let the wire come into contact with water.

3. Communication between people who are apart. (связь, общение)

  1. We lost contact with them after the move.
  2. She has had no contact with her father in years.

4. A thin lens worn on the eye instead of glasses, usually used in the plural. (контактная линза)

  1. I wear contacts during the week and glasses at night.
  2. One of my contacts fell out on the bus.
  3. She keeps a spare contact in her bag.
