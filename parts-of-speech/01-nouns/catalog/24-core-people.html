Core vocabulary: people, family, and work#

The everyday nouns for people — relatives, roles, and professions. Regular, countable, and no traps, which is exactly why they get a shorter entry.

← The grammar of nouns · All groups

Groups 1–23 cover the nouns that misbehave. This group and the ones after it cover the nouns you simply need to know: frequent, well-behaved words with regular plurals and no grammatical surprises.

Because there is no story to tell about the grammar of doctor, these entries are deliberately lean — pronunciation, Russian, plural, countability, a one-line gloss, and three examples. Where a word does hide something (suspect shifts its stress, plumber has a silent b, immigrant is the mirror image of emigrant), a Contrast line says so.

Nouns for people are overwhelmingly countable and take regular -s. The exceptions already have their own entries: man, woman, child, person, and the gender pairs.
