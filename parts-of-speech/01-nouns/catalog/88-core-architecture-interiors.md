# Core vocabulary: architecture and interiors

> The everyday nouns of architecture and interiors — pronunciation, Russian, plural, and three examples each.

[← The grammar of nouns](../README.md) &middot; [All groups](README.md)

Part of the **core-vocabulary tier**. Groups 1–23 cover the nouns that misbehave; this tier covers the nouns you simply need to know. Entries are deliberately lean, because a regular countable noun has no grammatical story to tell. A **Contrast** line appears only where the word hides a genuine trap.

Where a word in this field *does* misbehave, it lives in the grammar tier instead and is not repeated here.

### flooring

**Pronunciation:** /ˈflɔːrɪŋ/ &middot; *FLOR-ing*
**Русский:** напольное покрытие
**Countability:** uncountable
**Contrast:** Uncountable: 'new flooring', never 'a flooring' or 'floorings'.

The material that covers a floor.

1. We're replacing the *flooring* in the kitchen this spring.
2. Bamboo *flooring* has gotten popular in the last few years.
3. The *flooring* under the sink got soaked.

### hall

**Pronunciation:** /hɔːl/ &middot; *HAWL*
**Русский:** коридор; зал
**Plural:** *halls*
**Countability:** countable
**Contrast:** Homophone of 'haul'. In an American house 'hall' usually means the hallway; British speakers more often mean the entrance area.

### lighting

**Pronunciation:** /ˈlaɪtɪŋ/ &middot; *LY-ting*
**Русский:** освещение; освещение (сценическое)
**Countability:** uncountable
**Contrast:** Uncountable: 'the lighting', never 'lightings'.

Lighting has 2 distinct senses.

**1. The light in a room and the equipment that provides it.** (освещение)

1. The *lighting* in that restaurant is way too dim.
2. Good *lighting* makes a small room feel bigger.
3. We spent most of the budget on *lighting*.

**2. The arrangement of lights used to light a stage or a room.** (освещение (сценическое))

4. The *lighting* in the final scene is almost blue.
5. We spent the whole day working on *lighting*.
6. Bad *lighting* can ruin a good set.
