# Core vocabulary: restaurants and dining out

> The everyday nouns of restaurants and dining out — pronunciation, Russian, plural, and three examples each.

[← The grammar of nouns](../README.md) &middot; [All groups](README.md)

Part of the **core-vocabulary tier**. Groups 1–23 cover the nouns that misbehave; this tier covers the nouns you simply need to know. Entries are deliberately lean, because a regular countable noun has no grammatical story to tell. A **Contrast** line appears only where the word hides a genuine trap.

Where a word in this field *does* misbehave, it lives in the grammar tier instead and is not repeated here.

---
### carryout

**Pronunciation:** /ˈkɛriaʊt/ &middot; *KEHR-ee-owt*
**Русский:** еда навынос
**Countability:** uncountable
**Contrast:** Common in the Midwest; elsewhere Americans usually say takeout.

Restaurant food packed up to be eaten somewhere else.

1. Let's just get *carryout* tonight.
2. The place does mostly *carryout* now.
3. Their *carryout* is cheaper than ordering delivery.

### waitress

**Pronunciation:** /ˈweɪtrəs/ &middot; *WAY-truss*
**Русский:** официантка
**Plural:** *waitresses*
**Countability:** countable
**Contrast:** Most American restaurants now use the gender-neutral 'server' for the job.

A woman whose job is serving food and drinks at tables.

1. Our *waitress* brought extra napkins without being asked.
2. She worked as a *waitress* all through college.
3. Two *waitresses* were covering the whole dining room.

### main course

**Pronunciation:** /ˌmeɪn ˈkɔːrs/ &middot; *mayn-KORS*
**Русский:** основное блюдо, второе
**Plural:** *main courses*
**Countability:** countable
**Contrast:** Americans usually call this the entrée; in Britain an entrée is a starter.

The largest dish of a meal, served after the appetizer.

1. I had soup first and then a *main course* of grilled salmon.
2. Save room — the *main course* here is huge.
3. Both *main courses* came with mashed potatoes.

A smaller dish served alongside the main one.

### combo

### gratuity

**Русский:** чаевые
**Plural:** *gratuities*
**Countability:** countable
**Contrast:** The everyday word is 'tip'; gratuity is the formal one printed on checks and menus.
