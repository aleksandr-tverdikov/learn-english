Core vocabulary: household objects#

The everyday nouns of household objects — pronunciation, Russian, plural, and three examples each.

← The grammar of nouns · All groups

Part of the core-vocabulary tier. Groups 1–23 cover the nouns that misbehave; this tier covers the nouns you simply need to know. Entries are deliberately lean, because a regular countable noun has no grammatical story to tell. A Contrast line appears only where the word hides a genuine trap.

Where a word in this field does misbehave, it lives in the grammar tier instead and is not repeated here.

key#

Pronunciation
/kiː/ · KEE
Русский
ключ; ключ (к разгадке), главное условие; клавиша; легенда (карты), условные обозначения; тональность
Plural
keys
Countability
countable

Key has 5 distinct senses.

1. A shaped piece of metal that opens a lock. (ключ)

  1. I can't find my house keys.
  2. The key doesn't turn anymore.
  3. She left a spare key with the neighbors.

2. The thing that explains or solves something. (ключ (к разгадке), главное условие)

  1. Practice is the key to playing well.
  2. Sleep turned out to be the key to his recovery.
  3. That one letter was the key to the whole mystery.

3. One of the buttons you press on a keyboard, a piano, or a phone. (клавиша)

  1. The S key on my laptop sticks.
  2. He ran his fingers over the piano keys.
  3. Press any key to continue.

4. A list on a map or a chart explaining what its symbols mean. (легенда (карты), условные обозначения)

  1. The key shows that a green triangle is a campground.
  2. Read the key before you try to read the graph.
  3. Both maps use the same key.

5. The set of musical notes that a piece of music is built on. (тональность)

  1. The song is in the key of G.
  2. She sang it in a lower key to suit her voice.
  3. The two pieces are in different keys.
