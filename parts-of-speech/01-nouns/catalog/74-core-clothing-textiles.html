Core vocabulary: fabric and textiles#

The everyday nouns of fabric and textiles — pronunciation, Russian, plural, and three examples each.

← The grammar of nouns · All groups

Part of the core-vocabulary tier. Groups 1–23 cover the nouns that misbehave; this tier covers the nouns you simply need to know. Entries are deliberately lean, because a regular countable noun has no grammatical story to tell. A Contrast line appears only where the word hides a genuine trap.

Where a word in this field does misbehave, it lives in the grammar tier instead and is not repeated here.

textile#

Pronunciation
/ˈtɛkstaɪl/ · TEK-styl
Русский
текстиль, ткань
Plural
textiles
Countability
countable

A woven or knitted cloth, especially one made in a factory.

  1. The town's economy ran on textiles for a hundred years.
  2. She studies textile design at art school.
  3. Cheap imported textiles put the old mill out of business.

weave#

Pronunciation
/wiːv/ · WEEV
Русский
переплетение (нитей), плетение; накладные пряди, вплетённые волосы
Plural
weaves
Countability
countable

Weave has 2 distinct senses.

1. The way the threads of a fabric are crossed over each other. (переплетение (нитей), плетение)

  1. This shirt has a tight weave, so the wind doesn't get through.
  2. Hold it up to the light and you can see the weave.
  3. Linen and cotton take very different weaves.

2. Lengths of extra hair sewn or glued into a person's own hair. (накладные пряди, вплетённые волосы)

  1. She got a weave before the wedding.
  2. A good weave can last a couple of months.
  3. The salon does braids, weaves, and color.

Pronunciation

Русский
трикотаж, вязаное полотно
Plural
knits
Countability
countable
Contrast

A fabric or garment made by looping yarn rather than weaving it.

  1. In the fall she lives in soft knits and jeans.
  2. That sweater is a loose knit, so it stretches.
  3. The store keeps the knits on the back wall.

warp#

Pronunciation
/wɔːrp/ · WORP
Русский
коробление, искривление; основа (продольные нити ткани)
Countability
uncountable

Warp has 2 distinct senses.

1. A bend or twist in something that should be flat or straight. (коробление, искривление)

  1. There's a slight warp in the tabletop.
  2. Damp air put a warp in the door, and now it sticks.
  3. Sight down the board and check it for warp.

2. The lengthwise threads held on a loom while cloth is woven. (основа (продольные нити ткани))

  1. The warp runs the length of the fabric.
  2. It takes a whole day to set up the warp.
  3. A broken warp thread stops the machine.
