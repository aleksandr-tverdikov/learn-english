Core vocabulary: more nouns N-P#

Further everyday nouns, N-P — what a coverage check against WordNet showed was still missing.

← The grammar of nouns · All groups

Part of the core-vocabulary tier, gathered by checking the dictionary against a 40,940-word WordNet noun list and writing entries for what was genuinely absent.

Most of that list was not worth writing — proper nouns, Latin taxonomy, obsolete units and archaic terms — so these are the survivors of a deliberate rejection pass that threw out roughly six of every seven candidates, not the list itself.

  1. She got the nod to lead the project.
  2. The rookie got the nod to start on Sunday.
  3. The plan still needs a nod from the board.

3. A small detail that refers to or honors something else. (отсылка, дань уважения)

  1. The remake is full of nods to the original.
  2. The menu includes a nod to the owner's grandmother.
  3. That line is a clear nod to Shakespeare.
