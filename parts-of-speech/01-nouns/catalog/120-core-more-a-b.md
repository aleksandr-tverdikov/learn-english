# Core vocabulary: more nouns A-B

> Further everyday nouns, A-B — what a coverage check against WordNet showed was still missing.

[← The grammar of nouns](../README.md) &middot; [All groups](README.md)

Part of the **core-vocabulary tier**, gathered by checking the dictionary against a 40,940-word WordNet noun list and writing entries for what was genuinely absent.

Most of that list was not worth writing — proper nouns, Latin taxonomy, obsolete units and archaic terms — so these are the survivors of a deliberate rejection pass that threw out roughly six of every seven candidates, not the list itself.

### aardvark

**Pronunciation:** /ˈɑːrdvɑːrk/ &middot; *AHRD-vahrk*
**Русский:** трубкозуб
**Plural:** *aardvarks*
**Countability:** countable
**Contrast:** The double a at the start is one long sound, AHR — not two separate a's.

A burrowing African mammal with a long snout that eats ants and termites.

1. The zoo's *aardvark* sleeps all day and comes out at night.
2. *Aardvarks* use their long tongues to pull termites out of a mound.
3. In the alphabet book, A is for *aardvark*.

**Countability:** countable
