# Core vocabulary: education

> The everyday nouns of education — pronunciation, Russian, plural, and three examples each.

[← The grammar of nouns](../README.md) &middot; [All groups](README.md)

Part of the **core-vocabulary tier**. Groups 1–23 cover the nouns that misbehave; this tier covers the nouns you simply need to know. Entries are deliberately lean, because a regular countable noun has no grammatical story to tell. A **Contrast** line appears only where the word hides a genuine trap.

Where a word in this field *does* misbehave, it lives in the grammar tier instead and is not repeated here.

### university

**Pronunciation:** /ˌjuːnɪˈvɜːrsəti/ &middot; *yoo-nih-VUR-suh-tee*
**Русский:** университет
**Plural:** *universities*
**Countability:** countable

A place of higher education that grants degrees and does research.

1. She teaches physics at a state *university*.
2. He applied to four *universities* in the Midwest.
3. The *university* raised its fees again this year.

### college

**Pronunciation:** /ˈkɑːlɪdʒ/ &middot; *KAH-lij*
**Русский:** колледж, вуз
**Plural:** *colleges*
**Countability:** countable
**Contrast:** In the US, *college* normally means four-year higher education, and Americans say went to college where the British say went to university.

A school that students attend after high school to earn a degree.

1. Their oldest son starts *college* in the fall.
2. She went to a small *college* in Ohio.
3. Both *colleges* offered him financial aid.

### library

**Pronunciation:** /ˈlaɪbrɛri/ &middot; *LY-brer-ee*
**Русский:** библиотека; библиотека (программная)
**Plural:** *libraries*
**Countability:** countable
**Contrast:** Three syllables in careful American speech: LY-brair-ee. "LY-berry" is common but considered sloppy.

Library has 3 distinct senses.

**1. A building or room holding books that people can read or borrow.** (библиотека)

1. I study in the *library* between classes.
2. The *library* closes at ten on weeknights.
3. Two campus *libraries* stay open all night during finals.

**2. A building or room where books are kept and lent out.** (библиотека)

4. The *library* closes at six on Sundays.
5. I got the book from the public *library*.
6. The city runs eleven branch *libraries*.

**3. A collection of ready-made code that other programs can use.** (библиотека (программная))

7. There's a *library* that already does that.
8. The project depends on about sixty *libraries*.
9. That *library* hasn't been updated in years.

**2. A room or building where scientific work is done.** (лаборатория)

4. The samples went straight to the *laboratory*.
5. She runs a research *laboratory* at the university.
6. Both *laboratories* got the same result.
