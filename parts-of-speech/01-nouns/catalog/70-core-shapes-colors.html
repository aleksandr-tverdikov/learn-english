Core vocabulary: shapes and colors#

The everyday nouns of shapes and colors — pronunciation, Russian, plural, and three examples each.

← The grammar of nouns · All groups

Part of the core-vocabulary tier. Groups 1–23 cover the nouns that misbehave; this tier covers the nouns you simply need to know. Entries are deliberately lean, because a regular countable noun has no grammatical story to tell. A Contrast line appears only where the word hides a genuine trap.

Where a word in this field does misbehave, it lives in the grammar tier instead and is not repeated here.

shape#

Pronunciation
/ʃeɪp/ · SHAYP
Русский
форма, очертание; состояние, форма (физическая)
Plural
shapes
Countability
countable

Shape has 2 distinct senses.

1. The outer form of something, seen as an outline. (форма, очертание)

  1. The cookies were cut in the shape of stars.
  2. Draw three different shapes on the board.
  3. That cloud has a funny shape.

2. The condition that a person or thing is in. (состояние, форма (физическая))

  1. He's in good shape for a man of seventy.
  2. The car is in rough shape but it still runs.
  3. The roof is in better shape than we expected.

  1. He is well known in academic circles.
  2. She has a small circle of close friends.
  3. The story spread quickly through his circle.

line#

Pronunciation
/laɪn/ · LYNE
Русский
линия, черта; очередь; верёвка, шнур, леска; телефонная линия, связь; строка; реплика (актёра)
Plural
lines
Countability
countable

Line has 5 distinct senses.

1. A long thin mark on a surface. (линия, черта)

  1. Draw a straight line from corner to corner.
  2. Write your name on the dotted line.
  3. The parking lines had faded away.

2. A row of people or cars waiting their turn. (очередь)

  1. There was a line out the door at the bakery.
  2. We stood in line for forty minutes.
  3. The lines at the DMV are shorter on Wednesdays.

3. A length of rope, cord, or wire used for a purpose. (верёвка, шнур, леска)

  1. Hang the sheets on the line to dry.
  2. A fish snapped his line right at the boat.
  3. Crews strung new power lines along the highway.

4. A telephone connection. (телефонная линия, связь)

  1. The line went dead in the middle of the call.
  2. Please hold — I have another line ringing.
  3. We had two lines installed in the office.

5. A row of words in a text, or the words an actor speaks. (строка; реплика (актёра))

  1. Read the third line of the poem again.
  2. He forgot his lines on opening night.
  3. Her best line in the movie is only four words long.

4. A particular moment or stage in a process. (момент, этап)

  1. At that point I decided to quit.
  2. We reached a point where nobody trusted anyone.
  3. There's no turning back at this point.

arc#

Pronunciation

countable
Contrast

Arc has 3 distinct senses.

1. A curved line that forms part of a circle. (дуга)

  1. The ball rose in a high arc over the fence.
  2. Draw an arc with the compass.
  3. The sprinkler sprayed water in wide arcs.

2. The way a character or story develops from start to finish. (сюжетная арка, линия развития (персонажа))

  1. The show gives the villain a full redemption arc.
  2. Her character arc takes up the whole second season.
  3. The novel's emotional arc builds slowly toward the last chapter.

3. A flash of electricity that jumps across a gap. (электрическая дуга)

  1. A blue arc jumped between the two terminals.
  2. The welder struck an arc against the steel plate.
  3. Loose wiring can throw arcs that start a fire.

Side has 4 distinct senses.

  1. A triangle has three sides.

  1. The sandwich comes with a side of fries.
  2. I ordered coleslaw as my side.
  3. Their sides are better than their main dishes.

4. One branch of a family, traced through the mother or the father. (линия родства (по отцу, по матери))

  1. The red hair comes from my mother's side.
  2. Everyone on his father's side is tall.
  3. The two sides of the family met for the first time at the wedding.

Pronunciation

Blur has 2 distinct senses.

1. A shape that cannot be seen clearly because its edges are not sharp. (размытое пятно, расплывчатое изображение)

  1. Without my glasses everything is a blur.
  2. The car went by in a red blur.
  3. The photo shows two gray blurs in the background.

2. A stretch of time that you cannot remember clearly. (смутное воспоминание, время как в тумане)

  1. The first month with a newborn was a total blur.
  2. The whole trip is a blur now.
  3. Everything after the crash is a blur.

Mark has 4 distinct senses.

  1. The glass left a ring mark on the wood.
  2. There were muddy marks all over the floor.

  3. Water rose above the flood mark on the wall.
