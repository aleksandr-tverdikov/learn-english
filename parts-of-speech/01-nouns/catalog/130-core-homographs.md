# Core vocabulary: words spelled alike, said differently

> One spelling, two pronunciations, two meanings — *bass* the fish and *bass* the low notes are different words that happen to look the same.

[← The grammar of nouns](../README.md) &middot; [All groups](README.md)

Every other entry in this dictionary gives one pronunciation and lists its meanings underneath, because the meanings sound the same. These do not.

*Bass* the fish is /bæs/ and *bass* the low musical range is /beɪs/. *Lead* the metal is /lɛd/ and *lead* meaning first place is /liːd/. Treating those as one entry with two senses would attach a pronunciation to a meaning it does not have, so each gets its own entry and each points at the other.

Noun/verb pairs are deliberately excluded — *record*, *contrast*, *permit* shift stress between a noun and a verb, and this is a dictionary of nouns.

---
### alum

**Pronunciation:** /əˈlʌm/ &middot; *uh-LUM*
**Русский:** выпускник, выпускница (разг.)
**Plural:** *alums*
**Countability:** countable
**Contrast:** Spelled like *alum* /ˈæləm/, the chemical, but stressed on the second syllable — a different word.

An informal short word for a graduate of a school or a former member of a group.

1. She's an *alum* of the same high school as my brother.
2. The firm hires a lot of *alums* from that program.
3. Two *alums* came back to coach the team.

### alum

**Русский:** квасцы
**Countability:** uncountable
**Contrast:** Spelled like *alum* /əˈlʌm/, a graduate, but said AL-uhm with the stress up front — a different word.

1. A little *alum* keeps the pickles crisp.
2. He touched *alum* to the nick from shaving.

### bass

**Pronunciation:** /beɪs/ &middot; *BAYSS*
**Русский:** бас, низкие частоты
**Countability:** uncountable
**Contrast:** Spelled like *bass* /bæs/, the fish — a different word entirely. The fish has a zero plural (*two bass*); this one is regular (*two basses*).

The lowest range of musical sound.

1. Turn the *bass* down — the whole building can hear it.
2. You could feel the *bass* through the floor.
3. This mix has too much *bass* and no melody.

### bow

**Pronunciation:** /baʊ/ &middot; *BOW*
**Русский:** нос (корабля)
**Plural:** *bows*
**Countability:** countable
**Contrast:** Spelled like *bow* /boʊ/, a knot or the weapon that shoots arrows, but said BOW to rhyme with *cow* — a different word. Bending forward to an audience — *take a bow* — is this one too.

The front end of a ship or boat.

1. Waves broke over the *bow* all afternoon.
2. She stood at the *bow* watching for whales.
3. The *bows* of the old fishing boats were painted blue.

### chamois

**Pronunciation:** /ʃæmˈwɑː/ &middot; *sham-WAH*

**Plural:** *chamois*
**Countability:** countable
**Contrast:** Spelled like *chamois* /ˈʃæmi/, the polishing cloth, but said sham-WAH — a different word. The plural is spelled the same.

### content

**Pronunciation:** /kənˈtɛnt/ &middot; *kuhn-TENT*
**Русский:** довольство, удовлетворённость
**Countability:** uncountable
**Contrast:** Spelled like *content* /ˈkɑːntɛnt/, the material inside something, but stressed on the second syllable — a different word. Heard most often in *to one's heart's content*.

A calm feeling of being satisfied with what you have.

1. She read on the porch to her heart's *content*.
2. The kids ate ice cream to their heart's *content*.
3. There was a look of quiet *content* on his face.

### desert

**Pronunciation:** /dɪˈzɜːrt/ &middot; *dih-ZURT*
**Русский:** заслуженное, по заслугам
**Plural:** *deserts*
**Countability:** countable
**Contrast:** Spelled like *desert* /ˈdɛzərt/, the dry land, but stressed on the second syllable, like *dessert* — a different word. Almost always plural, in *just deserts*.

What a person deserves, whether reward or punishment.

1. The swindler finally got his just *deserts*.
2. Everyone in that story gets their just *deserts*.
3. The judge said the sentence was no more than his just *deserts*.

### primer

**Pronunciation:** /ˈpraɪmər/ &middot; *PRY-mur*
**Русский:** грунтовка
**Plural:** *primers*
**Countability:** countable, and uncountable as a substance

### sake
