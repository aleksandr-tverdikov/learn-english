Core vocabulary: sea life#

The everyday nouns of sea life — pronunciation, Russian, plural, and three examples each.

← The grammar of nouns · All groups

Part of the core-vocabulary tier. Groups 1–23 cover the nouns that misbehave; this tier covers the nouns you simply need to know. Entries are deliberately lean, because a regular countable noun has no grammatical story to tell. A Contrast line appears only where the word hides a genuine trap.

Where a word in this field does misbehave, it lives in the grammar tier instead and is not repeated here.

porpoise#

Pronunciation
/ˈpɔːrpəs/ · POR-puhs
Русский
морская свинья (китообразное)
Plural
porpoises
Countability
countable
Contrast
The ending is said like the word purpose, not 'poyz'.

A small sea mammal like a dolphin but shorter, with a blunt nose.

  1. Porpoises are stockier than dolphins and have blunter faces.
  2. We spotted a porpoise in the bay at dawn.
  3. The crew pulled in the nets to avoid catching a porpoise.

sea lion#

Pronunciation
/ˈsiː ˌlaɪrn/ · SEE-ly-uhn
Русский
морской лев
Plural
sea lions
Countability
countable

A large seal with visible ear flaps that can walk on its front flippers.

  1. The sea lions down at the pier are a tourist attraction.
  2. A sea lion can move surprisingly fast on land.
  3. You can hear the sea lions barking from three blocks away.

manatee#

Pronunciation
/ˈmænəti/ · MAN-uh-tee
Русский
ламантин
Plural
manatees
Countability
countable

A slow, heavy plant-eating sea mammal of warm shallow water.

  1. Manatees gather in the warm water near the power plant.
  2. A manatee can eat a tenth of its body weight in a day.
  3. We rented kayaks and paddled out to see the manatees.

narwhal#

Pronunciation
/ˈnɑːrwɑːl/ · NAR-wahl
Русский
нарвал
Plural
narwhals
Countability
countable
Contrast
The h is silent: NAR-wahl.

An Arctic whale whose long straight tusk sticks out from its head.

  1. A narwhal's tusk is really one giant tooth.
  2. Narwhals live under the Arctic ice year-round.
  3. Sailors used to sell narwhal tusks as unicorn horns.
