Core vocabulary: landscape and geography#

The everyday nouns of landscape and geography — pronunciation, Russian, plural, and three examples each.

← The grammar of nouns · All groups

Part of the core-vocabulary tier. Groups 1–23 cover the nouns that misbehave; this tier covers the nouns you simply need to know. Entries are deliberately lean, because a regular countable noun has no grammatical story to tell. A Contrast line appears only where the word hides a genuine trap.

Where a word in this field does misbehave, it lives in the grammar tier instead and is not repeated here.

mountain#

Pronunciation
/ˈmaʊntən/ · MOWN-tuhn
Русский
гора
Plural
mountains
Countability
countable

A very high, steep piece of land, much taller than a hill.

  1. We hiked to the top of the mountain before noon.
  2. The mountains were still covered in snow in May.
  3. There's a cabin about halfway up the mountain.

Pronunciation
/hɪl/ · HIL
Русский
холм, возвышенность
Plural
hills
Countability
countable

A raised area of land, lower and less steep than a mountain.

  1. Their house sits on a hill overlooking the river.
  2. The road climbs a series of steep hills.
  3. We sledded down the hill behind the school.

Pronunciation

Русский

Countability
countable

The low land between hills or mountains, often with a river running through it.

  1. The whole valley fills with fog in the early morning.
  2. Most of the farms in this valley grow corn.
  3. Two valleys meet just south of town.

A deep, narrow valley with steep rock walls, usually cut by a river.

  1. The trail drops a thousand feet into the canyon.
  2. You can hear your own echo across the canyon.
  3. Narrow canyons like this one flood fast when it rains upstream.

  3. We followed the ravine until it opened onto a meadow.

Pronunciation

uncountable

uncountable

land#

Pronunciation
/lænd/ · LAND
Русский
земля, суша
Countability
uncountable

The solid part of the earth's surface, as opposed to the sea.

  1. After six weeks at sea they finally sighted land.
  2. His family has farmed this land for a century.
  3. Most of the land east of the river is protected.

terrain#

Pronunciation
/təˈreɪn/ · tuh-RAYN
Русский
местность, рельеф
Countability
uncountable
Contrast
Stress falls on the second syllable: tuh-RAYN, not TER-rayn.

Land of a particular kind, thought of in terms of its physical features.

  1. The terrain gets rocky past the second bridge.
  2. This truck handles rough terrain well.
  3. Flat, open terrain makes the wind feel colder.

  1. The landscape changes completely once you cross the pass.
