Core vocabulary: landscape and geography#

The everyday nouns of landscape and geography — pronunciation, Russian, plural, and three examples each.

← The grammar of nouns · All groups

Part of the core-vocabulary tier. Groups 1–23 cover the nouns that misbehave; this tier covers the nouns you simply need to know. Entries are deliberately lean, because a regular countable noun has no grammatical story to tell. A Contrast line appears only where the word hides a genuine trap.

Where a word in this field does misbehave, it lives in the grammar tier instead and is not repeated here.

mountain#

Pronunciation
/ˈmaʊntən/ · MOWN-tuhn
Русский
гора; куча, груда, масса (чего-л.)
Plural
mountains
Countability
countable

Mountain has 2 distinct senses.

1. A very high, steep piece of land, much taller than a hill. (гора)

  1. We hiked to the top of the mountain before noon.
  2. The mountains were still covered in snow in May.
  3. There's a cabin about halfway up the mountain.

2. A very large pile or amount of something. (куча, груда, масса (чего-л.))

  1. There's a mountain of laundry on the bed.
  2. She came back from vacation to a mountain of email.
  3. They ran up mountains of debt paying for the treatment.

A raised area of land, lower and less steep than a mountain.

  1. Ticket sales hit their peak in early June.

Ridge has 2 distinct senses.

  1. The trail follows the ridge for about four miles.
  2. The wind hits hard once you're up on the ridge.

  1. Snow slid off both sides of the roof ridge.

  3. The chip has ridges so it won't break in the dip.

range#

Pronunciation
/reɪndʒ/ · RAYNJ
Русский
диапазон; ассортимент, круг (вопросов); горная цепь, хребет; кухонная плита; стрельбище; тренировочное поле для гольфа; дальность (хода, полёта, выстрела)
Plural
ranges
Countability
countable

Range has 5 distinct senses.

1. The space between the lowest and highest amounts, or the whole set of things covered. (диапазон; ассортимент, круг (вопросов))

  1. Houses here are well out of our price range.
  2. The store carries a wide range of hand tools.
  3. The class covers a broad range of topics.

2. A line or group of mountains or hills. (горная цепь, хребет)

  1. The range runs north to south for three hundred miles.
  2. Snow stays on the higher ranges into July.
  3. A storm was building over the coastal range.

3. A large kitchen appliance with burners on top and an oven below. (кухонная плита)

  1. She cooks on a six-burner gas range.
  2. The new range barely fit through the kitchen door.
  3. Wipe down the range after you fry anything.

4. An outdoor area set aside for shooting practice or for hitting golf balls. (стрельбище; тренировочное поле для гольфа)

  1. He spends Saturday mornings at the shooting range.
  2. We hit a bucket of balls at the driving range.
  3. The range is closed until the new backstop goes in.

5. The greatest distance something can travel or reach. (дальность (хода, полёта, выстрела))

  1. The electric car has a range of about 250 miles.
  2. The deer was well within range.
  3. These little radios have a range of two miles.

  3. She won the student council race by a landslide.

ground#

Pronunciation
/ɡraʊnd/ · GROWND
Русский
земля, поверхность земли; основание, причина, повод; территория вокруг здания, парк при усадьбе; кофейная гуща
Countability
uncountable

Ground has 4 distinct senses.

1. The solid surface of the earth that you walk on. (земля, поверхность земли)

  1. The ground was still frozen in early April.
  2. He dropped his keys on the ground.
  3. Roots spread out just under the ground.

2. A reason or basis for an action, a belief, or a decision. (основание, причина, повод)

  1. Lying on the application is grounds for dismissal.
  2. She had good grounds for the complaint.
  3. On what grounds was the case thrown out?

3. The land and gardens that belong to a large building. (территория вокруг здания, парк при усадьбе)

  1. Visitors are welcome to walk the hospital grounds.
  2. The grounds stay open until sunset.
  3. They held the wedding on the grounds of an old estate.

4. The wet bits of coffee left in the pot after brewing. (кофейная гуща)

  1. Dump the coffee grounds in the compost.
  2. There were grounds at the bottom of my cup.
  3. She spreads used grounds around the roses.

Pronunciation
/lænd/ · LAND
Русский
земля, суша; земля, земельный участок; страна, край
Countability

Land has 3 distinct senses.

1. The solid part of the earth's surface, as opposed to the sea. (земля, суша)

  1. After six weeks at sea they finally sighted land.
  2. His family has farmed this land for a century.
  3. Most of the land east of the river is protected.

2. Ground that someone owns, farms, or builds on. (земля, земельный участок)

  1. They bought forty acres of land outside town.
  2. His family has farmed this land for a century.
  3. The company owns land on both sides of the river.

3. A country or region. (страна, край)

  1. America is still called a land of opportunity.
  2. The story is set in a distant land.
  3. Her grandparents came from a land she has never seen.

landscape#

Pronunciation
/ˈlændskeɪp/ · LAND-skayp
Русский
пейзаж, ландшафт; положение дел, обстановка (в какой-либо сфере); пейзаж (картина, фотография); альбомная ориентация (страницы)
Plural
landscapes
Countability
countable

Landscape has 4 distinct senses.

1. Everything you can see when you look across an area of land. (пейзаж, ландшафт)

  1. The landscape changes completely once you cross the pass.
  2. She paints desert landscapes.
  3. Wind turbines have changed the landscape around here.

2. The whole set of conditions in an area of activity at a given time. (положение дел, обстановка (в какой-либо сфере))

  1. Streaming changed the landscape of the music business.
  2. The political landscape looks very different after the election.
  3. Small firms have to survive in a crowded competitive landscape.

3. A painting or photograph of country scenery. (пейзаж (картина, фотография))

  1. A small landscape hung above the fireplace.
  2. The museum's landscapes are mostly from the 1800s.
  3. He paints landscapes of the Hudson Valley.

4. The page format that is wider than it is tall. (альбомная ориентация (страницы))

  1. Print the spreadsheet in landscape so the columns fit.
  2. Switch the page to landscape before you add the chart.
  3. The photo works better in landscape than in portrait.

field#

Pronunciation
/fiːld/ · FEELD
Русский
поле; область, сфера деятельности; поле, площадка (спортивная); поле (в форме, базе данных); поле (магнитное, гравитационное)

Field has 5 distinct senses.

1. An open piece of land, usually used for growing crops or keeping animals. (поле)

  1. The field behind the barn is planted in soybeans.
  2. Cows were standing in the far field.
  3. We cut through a field to get back to the road.

2. A subject or area of work that someone specializes in. (область, сфера деятельности)

  1. She's a leading expert in her field.
  2. The conference brings together people from several fields.
  3. He left law for a field with better hours.

3. A marked area of ground where a sport is played. (поле, площадка (спортивная))

  1. The team took the field to a roar from the crowd.
  2. Rain left the field too muddy to play on.
  3. The high school built two new soccer fields.

4. A space on a form or screen where you type one piece of information. (поле (в форме, базе данных))

  1. Fill in every required field before you hit submit.
  2. Leave the middle name field blank.
  3. The database has separate fields for phone and email.

5. The area in which a force such as magnetism or gravity acts. (поле (магнитное, гравитационное))

  1. Iron filings show the shape of a magnetic field.
  2. The satellite measures the earth's gravitational field.
  3. Strong magnetic fields can wipe a hotel key card.
