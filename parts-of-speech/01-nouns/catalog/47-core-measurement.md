# Core vocabulary: measurement

> The everyday nouns of measurement — pronunciation, Russian, plural, and three examples each.

[← The grammar of nouns](../README.md) &middot; [All groups](README.md)

Part of the **core-vocabulary tier**. Groups 1–23 cover the nouns that misbehave; this tier covers the nouns you simply need to know. Entries are deliberately lean, because a regular countable noun has no grammatical story to tell. A **Contrast** line appears only where the word hides a genuine trap.

Where a word in this field *does* misbehave, it lives in the grammar tier instead and is not repeated here.

---
### measurement

**Pronunciation:** /ˈmeʒərmənt/ &middot; *MEZH-ur-muhnt*
**Русский:** измерение, замер
**Plural:** *measurements*
**Countability:** countable
**Contrast:** The s is a zh sound, as in pleasure: MEZH-ur-muhnt.

A figure showing the size, length, or amount of something.

1. Take a *measurement* before you cut the board.
2. The tailor wrote down all my *measurements*.
3. That *measurement* looks off by half an inch.

### unit

**Pronunciation:** /ˈjuːnɪt/ &middot; *YOO-nit*
**Русский:** единица измерения

**Pronunciation:** /ˈkɪləwɑːt/ &middot; *KIL-uh-waht*
**Русский:** киловатт
**Plural:** *kilowatts*
**Countability:** countable

One thousand watts.

1. The space heater draws two *kilowatts*.
2. The panels produce five *kilowatts* at noon.
3. Motors abroad are rated in *kilowatts*.
