# Core vocabulary: footwear

> The everyday nouns of footwear — pronunciation, Russian, plural, and three examples each.

[← The grammar of nouns](../README.md) &middot; [All groups](README.md)

Part of the **core-vocabulary tier**. Groups 1–23 cover the nouns that misbehave; this tier covers the nouns you simply need to know. Entries are deliberately lean, because a regular countable noun has no grammatical story to tell. A **Contrast** line appears only where the word hides a genuine trap.

Where a word in this field *does* misbehave, it lives in the grammar tier instead and is not repeated here.

---
### dress shoe

**Pronunciation:** /ˈdrɛs ʃuː/ &middot; *DRES shoo*
**Русский:** модельные туфли (к костюму)
**Plural:** *dress shoes*
**Countability:** countable

A formal leather shoe worn with a suit or with office clothes.

1. He polished his *dress shoes* the night before the interview.
2. You can't wear sneakers to a wedding, so bring a *dress shoe* or two.
3. Her *dress shoes* were killing her by the end of the reception.

### running shoe

**Pronunciation:** /ˈrʌnɪŋ ʃuː/ &middot; *RUN-ing shoo*
**Русский:** кроссовки для бега
**Plural:** *running shoes*
**Countability:** countable

A light, cushioned athletic shoe made for running.

1. I replace my *running shoes* every five hundred miles.
2. A good *running shoe* should feel snug in the heel.
3. She bought her first pair of real *running shoes* before the marathon.

### tennis shoe

**Pronunciation:** /ˈtɛnɪs ʃuː/ &middot; *TEN-is shoo*
**Русский:** кроссовки, кеды
**Plural:** *tennis shoes*
**Countability:** countable
**Contrast:** In much of the US *tennis shoes* means sneakers of any kind, not just shoes for tennis. British English says *trainers*.

A soft canvas or leather sports shoe with a rubber sole.

1. Grandma calls every sneaker a *tennis shoe*.
2. The kids tracked mud in with their *tennis shoes*.
3. Wear *tennis shoes* to the museum — there's a lot of walking.

### athletic shoe

**Pronunciation:** /æθˈlɛtɪk ʃuː/ &middot; *ath-LET-ik shoo*
**Русский:** спортивная обувь, кроссовки
**Plural:** *athletic shoes*
**Countability:** countable
**Contrast:** Athletic has three syllables: ath-LET-ik. Adding a fourth ("ath-uh-LET-ik") is a common mistake.

Any shoe designed for sports or exercise.

1. The store's whole back wall is *athletic shoes*.
2. Gym class requires a closed-toe *athletic shoe*.
3. *Athletic shoes* have gotten a lot more expensive.
