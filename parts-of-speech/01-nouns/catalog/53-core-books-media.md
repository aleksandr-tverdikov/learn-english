# Core vocabulary: books and media

> The everyday nouns of books and media — pronunciation, Russian, plural, and three examples each.

[← The grammar of nouns](../README.md) &middot; [All groups](README.md)

Part of the **core-vocabulary tier**. Groups 1–23 cover the nouns that misbehave; this tier covers the nouns you simply need to know. Entries are deliberately lean, because a regular countable noun has no grammatical story to tell. A **Contrast** line appears only where the word hides a genuine trap.

Where a word in this field *does* misbehave, it lives in the grammar tier instead and is not repeated here.

---
### book

**Pronunciation:** /bʊk/ &middot; *BUUK*
**Русский:** книга
**Plural:** *books*
**Countability:** countable

A set of printed or written pages bound together inside a cover.

1. I'm reading a *book* about the Civil War.
2. She keeps her *books* in alphabetical order.
3. The *book* came out last spring.

### page

**Pronunciation:** /peɪdʒ/ &middot; *PAYJ*
**Русский:** страница
**Plural:** *pages*
**Countability:** countable

One side of a sheet of paper in a book, magazine, or newspaper.

1. Turn to *page* forty-two.
2. The article runs about six *pages*.
3. He folded the corner of the *page* to mark his place.
