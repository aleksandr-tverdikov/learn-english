Core vocabulary: the body#

The everyday nouns of the body — pronunciation, Russian, plural, and three examples each.

body#

Pronunciation
/ˈbɑːdi/ · BAH-dee
Русский
тело; тело, труп; орган, организация (коллективный); масса, совокупность (воды, данных, работ)
Plural
bodies
Countability
countable

Body has 4 distinct senses.

1. The whole physical structure of a person or animal. (тело)

  1. Yoga taught me how my body actually works.
  2. Cold water is a shock to the body.
  3. Their bodies were still sore from Saturday's game.

2. A dead person. (тело, труп)

  1. Hikers found a body at the bottom of the ravine.
  2. The body was identified from dental records.
  3. Two bodies were recovered from the wreck.

3. An organized group of people acting together officially. (орган, организация (коллективный))

  1. The governing body meets twice a year.
  2. The student body voted on the new rules.
  3. An independent body will review the complaints.

4. A large mass or collection of something. (масса, совокупность (воды, данных, работ))

  1. The lake is the largest body of water in the state.
  2. There's a growing body of evidence on the subject.
  3. Her body of work spans forty years.

arm#

Pronunciation
/ɑːrm/ · AHRM
Русский
рука (от плеча до кисти); оружие, вооружение; подразделение, отделение (организации); подлокотник
Plural
arms
Countability
countable
Contrast
Russian рука covers the whole limb; English splits it — the arm ends at the wrist, and the hand takes over from there.

Arm has 4 distinct senses.

1. The limb that runs from the shoulder down to the hand. (рука (от плеча до кисти))

  1. He broke his arm falling off a ladder.
  2. She carried the groceries in both arms.
  3. Put your arm around her for the photo.

2. Weapons, especially guns, spoken of as a group. (оружие, вооружение)

  1. The Constitution mentions the right to bear arms.
  2. Both countries agreed to limit nuclear arms.
  3. The rebels laid down their arms at dawn.

3. A branch or division of a larger organization. (подразделение, отделение (организации))

  1. Research is the fastest-growing arm of the company.
  2. The charity's legal arm handles evictions.
  3. Congress created the agency as an investigative arm.

4. The side part of a chair or sofa that supports your arm. (подлокотник)

  1. He gripped the arms of the chair and stood up.
  2. The cat sleeps on the arm of the couch.
  3. One arm of the recliner is coming loose.

hand#

Pronunciation
/hænd/ · HAND
Русский
рука, кисть руки; стрелка (часов); карты на руках; партия (в картах); работник, подсобный рабочий; матрос; помощь
Plural
hands
Countability
countable
Contrast
Never use hand for the whole рука — you hold a bag in your hand but carry a child in your arms.

Hand has 5 distinct senses.

1. The part of the body below the wrist, with fingers and a thumb. (рука, кисть руки)

  1. Wash your hands before dinner.
  2. He shook my hand and introduced himself.
  3. Her hands were freezing without gloves.

2. A pointer that moves around the face of a clock or a dial. (стрелка (часов))

  1. The minute hand is stuck.
  2. Both hands pointed straight up at noon.
  3. The second hand swept past the twelve.

3. The cards dealt to one player, or one round of a card game. (карты на руках; партия (в картах))

  1. He was dealt a terrible hand.
  2. Let's play one more hand before dinner.
  3. She won three hands in a row.

4. A worker who does physical labor on a farm, a ranch, or a ship. (работник, подсобный рабочий; матрос)

  1. They hired two extra hands for the harvest.
  2. He worked as a ranch hand one summer.
  3. All hands were called up on deck.

5. Help with a task. (помощь)

  1. Can you give me a hand with this couch?
  2. She could use a hand in the kitchen.
  3. Thanks for lending a hand.
