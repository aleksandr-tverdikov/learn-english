# Core vocabulary: war and the military

> The everyday nouns of war and the military — pronunciation, Russian, plural, and three examples each.

[← The grammar of nouns](../README.md) &middot; [All groups](README.md)

Part of the **core-vocabulary tier**. Groups 1–23 cover the nouns that misbehave; this tier covers the nouns you simply need to know. Entries are deliberately lean, because a regular countable noun has no grammatical story to tell. A **Contrast** line appears only where the word hides a genuine trap.

Where a word in this field *does* misbehave, it lives in the grammar tier instead and is not repeated here.

---
### warfare

**Pronunciation:** /ˈwɔːrfɛr/ &middot; *WAWR-fair*
**Русский:** военные действия, ведение войны
**Countability:** uncountable

The activity of fighting a war, or a particular way of fighting one.

1. Trench *warfare* defined the First World War.
2. The rebels turned to guerrilla *warfare*.
3. Chemical *warfare* is banned by treaty.

### combat

**Pronunciation:** /ˈkɑːmbæt/ &middot; *KAHM-bat*
**Русский:** бой, боевые действия
**Countability:** uncountable
**Contrast:** The noun is stressed on the first syllable, KAHM-bat; the verb to combat is kuhm-BAT.
