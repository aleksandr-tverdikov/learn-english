Core vocabulary: education#

Counselor has 3 distinct senses.

  1. My guidance counselor helped me pick a college.

  1. She spent three summers as a counselor at a camp in Maine.
  2. Our cabin counselor woke us at seven every morning.
  3. The camp is still hiring counselors for June.

3. A lawyer, especially one arguing a case in court. (адвокат, защитник (обращение в суде))

  1. Counselor, approach the bench.
  2. Both counselors met with the judge in chambers.
  3. The defendant conferred quietly with his counselor.
