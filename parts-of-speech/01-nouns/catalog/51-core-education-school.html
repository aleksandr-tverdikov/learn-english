Core vocabulary: education#

The everyday nouns of education — pronunciation, Russian, plural, and three examples each.

← The grammar of nouns · All groups

Part of the core-vocabulary tier. Groups 1–23 cover the nouns that misbehave; this tier covers the nouns you simply need to know. Entries are deliberately lean, because a regular countable noun has no grammatical story to tell. A Contrast line appears only where the word hides a genuine trap.

Where a word in this field does misbehave, it lives in the grammar tier instead and is not repeated here.

university#

Pronunciation
/ˌjuːnɪˈvɜːrsəti/ · yoo-nih-VUR-suh-tee
Русский
университет
Plural
universities
Countability
countable

A place of higher education that grants degrees and does research.

  1. She teaches physics at a state university.
  2. He applied to four universities in the Midwest.
  3. The university raised its fees again this year.

college#

Pronunciation
/ˈkɑːlɪdʒ/ · KAH-lij
Русский
колледж, вуз
Plural
colleges
Countability
countable
Contrast
In the US, college normally means four-year higher education, and Americans say went to college where the British say went to university.

A school that students attend after high school to earn a degree.

  1. Their oldest son starts college in the fall.
  2. She went to a small college in Ohio.
  3. Both colleges offered him financial aid.
