Core vocabulary: sport and games#

The everyday nouns of sport and games — pronunciation, Russian, plural, and three examples each.

← The grammar of nouns · All groups

Part of the core-vocabulary tier. Groups 1–23 cover the nouns that misbehave; this tier covers the nouns you simply need to know. Entries are deliberately lean, because a regular countable noun has no grammatical story to tell. A Contrast line appears only where the word hides a genuine trap.

Where a word in this field does misbehave, it lives in the grammar tier instead and is not repeated here.

sport#

Pronunciation
/spɔːrt/ · SPORT
Русский
спорт, вид спорта
Plural
sports
Countability
countable
Contrast
Americans say sports where the British say sport: he plays sports vs. he plays sport.

A physical activity played by rules for competition or fun.

  1. Soccer is the only sport he really follows.
  2. The school offers a dozen different sports in the fall.
  3. Swimming is a good sport for people with bad knees.

game#

Pronunciation
/ɡeɪm/ · GAYM
Русский
игра, матч
Plural
games
Countability
countable

A contest played by set rules, or one single contest in a series.

  1. We won the game by three points.
  2. There are two games left in the season.
  3. Chess is a game of patience.

  3. He lost the match in straight sets.

soccer#

Pronunciation
/ˈsɑːkər/ · SAH-kur
Русский
футбол
Countability
uncountable
Contrast
Soccer is the American name for the game the rest of the world calls football.

The game played on a large field by two sides of eleven who move the ball mostly with their feet.

  1. My daughter plays soccer on Saturdays.
  2. Soccer is finally getting decent TV ratings here.
  3. He coached youth soccer for ten years.

Pronunciation
/ˈfʊtbɔːl/ · FUUT-bawl

Plural

Countability

Contrast
In the US football means American football; the game played with the feet is soccer.

The American game played with an oval ball and helmets, or the ball used in it.

  1. He played football in high school.
  2. Somebody left two footballs out in the rain.
  3. Football season starts in September.
