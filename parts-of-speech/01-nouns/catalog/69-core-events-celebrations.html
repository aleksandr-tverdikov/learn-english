Core vocabulary: events and celebrations#

The everyday nouns of events and celebrations — pronunciation, Russian, plural, and three examples each.

← The grammar of nouns · All groups

Part of the core-vocabulary tier. Groups 1–23 cover the nouns that misbehave; this tier covers the nouns you simply need to know. Entries are deliberately lean, because a regular countable noun has no grammatical story to tell. A Contrast line appears only where the word hides a genuine trap.

Where a word in this field does misbehave, it lives in the grammar tier instead and is not repeated here.

bridesmaid#

Pronunciation
/ˈbraɪdzmeɪd/ · BRYDZ-mayd
Русский
подружка невесты
Plural
bridesmaids
Countability
countable

A woman who stands with the bride during a wedding and helps her prepare for it.

  1. She asked her sister to be a bridesmaid.
  2. All six bridesmaids wore navy blue.
  3. Being a bridesmaid turned out to be more work than I expected.

groom#

Pronunciation
/ɡruːm/ · GROOM
Русский
жених (на свадьбе); конюх
Plural
grooms
Countability
countable

Groom has 2 distinct senses.

1. The man who is getting married on his wedding day. (жених (на свадьбе))

  1. The groom looked nervous at the altar.
  2. The bride and groom cut the cake together.
  3. Traditionally the groom doesn't see the dress beforehand.

2. A person whose job is to feed and take care of horses. (конюх)

  1. The groom led the mare back to her stall.
  2. He worked as a groom at a racing stable.
  3. Two grooms were brushing the horses down.

countable

Registry has 2 distinct senses.

  1. Their registry is at Target if you want to send something.

  1. His name showed up on the state sex offender registry.
  2. She signed up for the national bone marrow registry.
  3. County registries hold every deed going back a century.

ball#

Pronunciation
/bɔːl/ · BAWL
Русский
мяч; шар, комок, клубок; бал
Plural
balls
Countability
countable

Ball has 3 distinct senses.

1. A round object that is thrown, kicked, or hit in games. (мяч)

  1. The dog brought the ball back every time.
  2. He hit the ball over the fence.
  3. We lost three balls in the tall grass.

2. Something squeezed or rolled into a round mass. (шар, комок, клубок)

  1. Roll the dough into a ball and let it rest.
  2. The cat unwound a whole ball of yarn.
  3. He crushed the letter into a ball.

3. A large formal party with dancing. (бал)

  1. The charity ball is black tie.
  2. She's been to two balls this season.
  3. The governor's inaugural ball was held downtown.

dance#

Pronunciation
/dæns/ · DANSS
Русский
танцевальный вечер, танцы; танец (как искусство), танцы; танец (конкретный)
Plural
dances
Countability
countable

Dance has 3 distinct senses.

1. A social event where people come to dance. (танцевальный вечер, танцы)

  1. There's a dance at the school gym on Friday.
  2. She went to every dance her senior year.
  3. The fire department holds a dance every spring.

2. The art or activity of moving the body in time to music. (танец (как искусство), танцы)

  1. She studied dance for twelve years.
  2. Modern dance was his first love.
  3. The school cut dance from the curriculum.

3. A particular set of steps performed to a piece of music. (танец (конкретный))

  1. The waltz is a slow dance in three-quarter time.
  2. They learned three folk dances before the trip.
  3. He asked her for one last dance.

theme#

Pronunciation
/θiːm/ · THEEM
Русский
тема, основная мысль; тема (оформления праздника); музыкальная тема, заглавная мелодия
Plural
themes
Countability
countable

Theme has 3 distinct senses.

1. The main idea that runs through a book, a film, or a discussion. (тема, основная мысль)

  1. Loneliness is the main theme of the novel.
  2. Several themes came up again and again in the interviews.
  3. The theme of her talk was second chances.

2. The subject or style that an event's decorations and costumes follow. (тема (оформления праздника))

  1. The party has a 1920s theme.
  2. Her birthday themes have gotten more elaborate every year.
  3. Pick a theme and the decorations get a lot easier.

3. A tune played again and again in a film, a show, or a piece of music. (музыкальная тема, заглавная мелодия)

  1. Everyone recognizes the theme from that movie.
  2. The show's opening theme is only fifteen seconds long.
  3. He hummed the theme all the way home.
