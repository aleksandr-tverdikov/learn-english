# Core vocabulary: cleaning and laundry

> The everyday nouns of cleaning and laundry — pronunciation, Russian, plural, and three examples each.

[← The grammar of nouns](../README.md) &middot; [All groups](README.md)

Part of the **core-vocabulary tier**. Groups 1–23 cover the nouns that misbehave; this tier covers the nouns you simply need to know. Entries are deliberately lean, because a regular countable noun has no grammatical story to tell. A **Contrast** line appears only where the word hides a genuine trap.

Where a word in this field *does* misbehave, it lives in the grammar tier instead and is not repeated here.

### mess

**Pronunciation:** /mɛs/ &middot; *MES*
**Русский:** беспорядок, бардак
**Plural:** *messes*
**Countability:** countable

A dirty or untidy state of a place or thing.

1. The kitchen was a *mess* after the party.
2. Clean up your *mess* before you go out.
3. The kids make three *messes* a day.

### clutter

**Pronunciation:** /ˈklʌtər/ &middot; *KLUT-ur*
**Русский:** хлам, захламлённость
**Countability:** uncountable
**Contrast:** Uncountable: *a lot of clutter*, never *a clutter* or *clutters*.

A lot of small things lying around where they do not belong.

1. The counter disappeared under all the *clutter*.
2. She spent Sunday getting rid of *clutter*.
3. There's too much *clutter* in this room.
