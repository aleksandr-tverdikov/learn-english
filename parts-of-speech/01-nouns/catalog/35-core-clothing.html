Core vocabulary: clothing#

The everyday nouns of clothing — pronunciation, Russian, plural, and three examples each.

← The grammar of nouns · All groups

Part of the core-vocabulary tier. Groups 1–23 cover the nouns that misbehave; this tier covers the nouns you simply need to know. Entries are deliberately lean, because a regular countable noun has no grammatical story to tell. A Contrast line appears only where the word hides a genuine trap.

Where a word in this field does misbehave, it lives in the grammar tier instead and is not repeated here.

Pronunciation
/ʃɜːrt/ · SHURT
Русский
рубашка

Countability
countable

A garment for the upper body, usually with a collar and buttons down the front.

Jacket has 2 distinct senses.

Plural

Countability
countable

Coat has 3 distinct senses.

1. A long outer garment worn over other clothes for warmth. (пальто)

  1. She hung her coat by the door.
  2. The coats are in the front closet.
  3. You'll need a heavier coat in Chicago.

2. A layer of paint or another substance spread over a surface. (слой (краски, лака))

  1. The door needs a second coat of paint.
  2. Give the table a thin coat of varnish.
  3. Two coats should cover the old color.

3. The fur or hair that covers an animal's body. (шерсть, шкура (животного))

  1. The dog has a thick winter coat.
  2. Brush the horse's coat until it shines.
  3. Their coats turn white in the fall.

suit#

Pronunciation
/suːt/ · SOOT
Русский
костюм (деловой); масть (в картах); иск, судебное дело; начальство, «пиджаки»
Plural
suits
Countability
countable
Contrast
Say SOOT in American English, and don't confuse it with suite /swiːt/, a set of rooms.

Suit has 4 distinct senses.

1. A set of matching jacket and pants or skirt worn together. (костюм (деловой))

  1. He wore a dark gray suit to the wedding.
  2. Her suits are all custom made.
  3. You don't need a suit for this interview.

2. One of the four sets a deck of playing cards is divided into. (масть (в картах))

  1. You have to follow suit if you can.
  2. Hearts is the strongest suit this round.
  3. Sort your cards by suit first.

3. A case brought against someone in a court of law. (иск, судебное дело)

  1. They filed a suit against the landlord.
  2. The suit was settled out of court.
  3. Three former workers joined the suit.

4. An executive or manager, seen as distant from ordinary workers. (начальство, «пиджаки»)

  1. The suits upstairs killed the project.
  2. A couple of suits from headquarters showed up.
  3. He quit the band and became a suit.
