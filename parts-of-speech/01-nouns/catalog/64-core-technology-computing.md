# Core vocabulary: technology and computing

> The everyday nouns of technology and computing — pronunciation, Russian, plural, and three examples each.

[← The grammar of nouns](../README.md) &middot; [All groups](README.md)

Part of the **core-vocabulary tier**. Groups 1–23 cover the nouns that misbehave; this tier covers the nouns you simply need to know. Entries are deliberately lean, because a regular countable noun has no grammatical story to tell. A **Contrast** line appears only where the word hides a genuine trap.

Where a word in this field *does* misbehave, it lives in the grammar tier instead and is not repeated here.

### app

**Pronunciation:** /æp/ &middot; *AP*
**Русский:** приложение
**Plural:** *apps*
**Countability:** countable

A program you run on a phone, tablet, or computer.

1. I downloaded a banking *app* last night.
2. There are too many *apps* on my phone.
3. The weather *app* says it's going to rain.

### network

**Pronunciation:** /ˈnɛtwɜːrk/ &middot; *NET-wurk*

7. Most jobs come through your *network*.
8. She built a *network* of clients over ten years.
9. The alumni *network* is worth joining.

**4. A system of roads, rails, or lines that cross and connect.** (сеть (дорог, линий))

10. The city's bus *network* runs until midnight.
11. The storm knocked out part of the power *network*.
12. A dense *network* of trails covers the park.
