# Core vocabulary: containers and packaging

> The everyday nouns of containers and packaging — pronunciation, Russian, plural, and three examples each.

[← The grammar of nouns](../README.md) &middot; [All groups](README.md)

Part of the **core-vocabulary tier**. Groups 1–23 cover the nouns that misbehave; this tier covers the nouns you simply need to know. Entries are deliberately lean, because a regular countable noun has no grammatical story to tell. A **Contrast** line appears only where the word hides a genuine trap.

Where a word in this field *does* misbehave, it lives in the grammar tier instead and is not repeated here.

### cardboard box

**Pronunciation:** /ˈkɑːrdbɔːrd bɑːks/ &middot; *KARD-bord BAHKS*
**Русский:** картонная коробка
**Plural:** *cardboard boxes*
**Countability:** countable

A box made of stiff layered paper, used for packing and moving things.

1. We packed the books into a *cardboard box*.
2. Empty *cardboard boxes* are stacked in the garage.
3. Flatten the *cardboard box* before you put it out for recycling.

### plastic bag

**Pronunciation:** /ˈplæstɪk bæɡ/ &middot; *PLAS-tik-bag*
**Русский:** полиэтиленовый пакет
**Plural:** *plastic bags*
**Countability:** countable
**Contrast:** American; in Britain the store version is a carrier bag.

Plastic bag has 2 distinct senses.

**1. A thin light bag made of plastic film for carrying or storing things.** (полиэтиленовый пакет)

1. She lined the trash can with a *plastic bag*.
2. The store charges ten cents for a *plastic bag*.
3. I keep a wad of *plastic bags* under the sink.

**2. A thin bag from a store, often charged for or banned.** (полиэтиленовый пакет)

4. The state banned single-use *plastic bags*.
5. There's a *plastic bag* full of *plastic bags* under the sink.
6. They charge ten cents for a *plastic bag*.
