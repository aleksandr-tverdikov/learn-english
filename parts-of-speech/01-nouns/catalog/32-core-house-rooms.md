# Core vocabulary: the house

> The everyday nouns of the house — pronunciation, Russian, plural, and three examples each.

[← The grammar of nouns](../README.md) &middot; [All groups](README.md)

Part of the **core-vocabulary tier**. Groups 1–23 cover the nouns that misbehave; this tier covers the nouns you simply need to know. Entries are deliberately lean, because a regular countable noun has no grammatical story to tell. A **Contrast** line appears only where the word hides a genuine trap.

Where a word in this field *does* misbehave, it lives in the grammar tier instead and is not repeated here.

---
### house

**Pronunciation:** /haʊs/ &middot; *HOWS*
**Русский:** дом (строение)
**Plural:** *houses*
**Countability:** countable
**Contrast:** The plural *houses* is pronounced HOW-ziz — the s turns into a z sound.

A building that people live in, usually one family per building.

1. They bought a *house* on the north side of town.
2. All the *houses* on this street were built in the fifties.
3. Come over to my *house* after work.

### home

**Pronunciation:** /hoʊm/ &middot; *HOHM*
**Русский:** дом (жильё), родной дом
**Plural:** *homes*
**Countability:** countable
**Contrast:** A *house* is the building; a *home* is where you live. And you go home, never "to home".

The place where someone lives and feels they belong.

1. I work from *home* three days a week.
2. They're saving up for their first *home*.
3. It took him two hours to get *home* in the snow.

### closet

**Pronunciation:** /ˈklɑːzɪt/ &middot; *KLAH-zit*

**Plural:** *closets*
**Countability:** countable

**2. A small storage room or recess in a wall, usually for clothes.** (встроенный шкаф, гардеробная)

4. Hang your coat in the hall *closet*.
5. This apartment has almost no *closet* space.
6. The bedroom *closets* are surprisingly deep.
