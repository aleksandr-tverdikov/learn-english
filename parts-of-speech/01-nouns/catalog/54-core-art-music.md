# Core vocabulary: art and music

> The everyday nouns of art and music — pronunciation, Russian, plural, and three examples each.

[← The grammar of nouns](../README.md) &middot; [All groups](README.md)

Part of the **core-vocabulary tier**. Groups 1–23 cover the nouns that misbehave; this tier covers the nouns you simply need to know. Entries are deliberately lean, because a regular countable noun has no grammatical story to tell. A **Contrast** line appears only where the word hides a genuine trap.

Where a word in this field *does* misbehave, it lives in the grammar tier instead and is not repeated here.

### gallery

**Pronunciation:** /ˈɡæləri/ &middot; *GAL-uh-ree*
**Русский:** галерея
**Plural:** *galleries*
**Countability:** countable

A room or building where works of art are shown, often for sale.

1. The *gallery* on Fifth Street shows local painters.
2. We spent the whole afternoon walking through three *galleries*.
3. Her work hangs in a small *gallery* downtown.

### exhibition

**Pronunciation:** /ˌɛksəˈbɪʃən/ &middot; *ek-suh-BISH-uhn*
**Русский:** выставка
**Plural:** *exhibitions*
**Countability:** countable
**Contrast:** The h is silent here: ek-suh-BISH-uhn. In the related noun *exhibit* the x sounds like gz and the h is heard: ig-ZIB-it.

A public show of paintings, photographs, or other objects.

1. The museum is putting on a big Picasso *exhibition* this fall.
2. Tickets for the *exhibition* sold out in a week.
3. Both *exhibitions* close at the end of the month.

### exhibit

**Pronunciation:** /ɪɡˈzɪbɪt/ &middot; *ig-ZIB-it*
**Русский:** экспонат; выставка (амер.)
**Plural:** *exhibits*
**Countability:** countable
**Contrast:** Americans often call a whole show an *exhibit*, where the British would say *exhibition*.

An object on display in a museum, or a whole display of such objects.

1. There's a new dinosaur *exhibit* at the science museum.
2. Each *exhibit* has a small card explaining it.
3. The *exhibits* are arranged by century.
