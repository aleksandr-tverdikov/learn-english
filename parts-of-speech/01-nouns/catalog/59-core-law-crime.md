# Core vocabulary: law and crime

> The everyday nouns of law and crime — pronunciation, Russian, plural, and three examples each.

[← The grammar of nouns](../README.md) &middot; [All groups](README.md)

Part of the **core-vocabulary tier**. Groups 1–23 cover the nouns that misbehave; this tier covers the nouns you simply need to know. Entries are deliberately lean, because a regular countable noun has no grammatical story to tell. A **Contrast** line appears only where the word hides a genuine trap.

Where a word in this field *does* misbehave, it lives in the grammar tier instead and is not repeated here.

---
### court

**Pronunciation:** /kɔːrt/ &middot; *KORT*
**Русский:** суд
**Plural:** *courts*
**Countability:** countable

The place where a judge decides legal cases, or the body of officials who decide them.

1. The case goes to *court* on Monday.
2. She spent two days in *court* as a witness.
3. Higher *courts* have already ruled on this question.

### attorney

**Pronunciation:** /əˈtɜːrni/ &middot; *uh-TUR-nee*
**Русский:** адвокат, юрист
**Plural:** *attorneys*
**Countability:** countable
**Contrast:** Americans normally say *attorney* or *lawyer*; the British say *solicitor* or *barrister*.

A person licensed to give legal advice and represent people in court.

1. You should talk to an *attorney* before signing.

### damages

**Pronunciation:** /ˈdæmɪdʒɪz/ &middot; *DAM-ih-jiz*
**Русский:** возмещение убытков, компенсация ущерба
