Core vocabulary: drinks#

The everyday nouns of drinks — pronunciation, Russian, plural, and three examples each.

← The grammar of nouns · All groups

Part of the core-vocabulary tier. Groups 1–23 cover the nouns that misbehave; this tier covers the nouns you simply need to know. Entries are deliberately lean, because a regular countable noun has no grammatical story to tell. A Contrast line appears only where the word hides a genuine trap.

Where a word in this field does misbehave, it lives in the grammar tier instead and is not repeated here.

drink#

Pronunciation
/drɪŋk/ · DRINGK
Русский
напиток; выпивка
Plural
drinks
Countability
both — countable and uncountable with different meanings

A liquid you swallow, especially one served in a glass or a cup.

  1. Can I get you a drink while you wait?
  2. They serve food and drinks until midnight.
  3. No food or drink is allowed past this point.

Plural

A drink made by steeping or fermenting something, especially coffee or beer.

  1. She put on a fresh brew before the meeting.
  2. The bar has a dozen local brews on the menu.
  3. That's a strong brew — I'd add some milk.

refill#

Pronunciation
/ˈriːfɪl/ · REE-fil
Русский
добавка, вторая порция; повторная выдача лекарства по рецепту; сменный блок, запасной стержень, картридж
Plural
refills
Countability
countable
Contrast
The stress moves: the noun is REE-fil, the verb is ree-FIL.

Refill has 3 distinct senses.

1. Another serving of a drink poured into the same cup or glass. (добавка, вторая порция)

  1. Would you like a refill on your coffee?
  2. Refills are free, so help yourself.
  3. He asked for a refill and went back to his book.

2. A further supply of a prescription medicine, given without a new prescription. (повторная выдача лекарства по рецепту)

  1. The prescription comes with two refills.
  2. I called the pharmacy for a refill.
  3. You're out of refills until you see the doctor again.

3. A replacement part or container for a product you already own. (сменный блок, запасной стержень, картридж)

  1. The pen takes a standard refill.
  2. Buy the soap refill; it's cheaper than a new bottle.
  3. Refills for that razor are hard to find.

Pronunciation
/θɜːrst/ · THURST
Русский
жажда; жажда (чего-либо), стремление
Countability

Thirst has 2 distinct senses.

1. The feeling of needing something to drink. (жажда)

  1. Nothing quenches thirst like cold water on a hot day.
  2. Salty food only makes your thirst worse.
  3. By the end of the hike, thirst was all anyone talked about.

2. A strong wish for something. (жажда (чего-либо), стремление)

  1. She has a real thirst for adventure.
  2. His thirst for knowledge never faded.
  3. There's a growing thirst for change in the district.

pop#

Pronunciation
/pɑːp/ · PAHP
Русский
газировка (в северных штатах); поп-музыка; хлопок (звук)
Plural
pops
Countability
countable, and uncountable as food
Contrast
Regional: the Midwest says pop, the coasts say soda, and much of the South says coke.

Pop has 3 distinct senses.

1. A fizzy soft drink, in Midwestern and Northwestern speech. (газировка (в северных штатах))

  1. Grab me a pop while you're up.
  2. In Michigan they call it pop, not soda.
  3. There's a cooler full of pops on the porch.

2. A style of popular music with catchy tunes, aimed at a wide audience. (поп-музыка)

  1. She grew up listening to eighties pop.
  2. The station plays nothing but pop.
  3. His new album leans more toward pop than country.

3. A short sharp sound like a cork coming out of a bottle. (хлопок (звук))

  1. The cork came out with a loud pop.
  2. We heard three pops and then silence.
  3. The balloon burst with a pop.

draft#

Pronunciation
/dræft/ · DRAFT
Русский
разливное пиво; черновик, проект (документа); сквозняк; драфт (набор игроков); призыв на военную службу
Countability
uncountable
Contrast
Americans spell it draft, the British draught; both sound the same.

Draft has 4 distinct senses.

1. Beer served from a keg rather than from a bottle or can. (разливное пиво)

  1. They've got eight beers on draft.
  2. Is the IPA on draft or only in bottles?
  3. Draft always tastes fresher to me.

2. An early version of a piece of writing or a plan. (черновик, проект (документа))

  1. The first draft of the report is due Friday.
  2. He is on the fourth draft of the novel.
  3. Lawyers reviewed a draft of the contract.

3. A current of cool air moving through a room. (сквозняк)

  1. There's a draft coming from under the door.
  2. Close the window; I feel a draft.
  3. Old houses are full of drafts.

4. The system by which sports teams pick new players, or by which a country calls people into the army. (драфт (набор игроков); призыв на военную службу)

  1. He was taken third overall in the draft.
  2. Her grandfather was called up in the draft.
  3. Both quarterbacks entered the draft a year early.

Pronunciation

Русский
бочка; ствол (оружия); баррель (мера нефти)
Plural

Countability

Barrel has 3 distinct senses.

1. A large round wooden container used to age wine or whiskey. (бочка)

  1. The whiskey sat in oak barrels for twelve years.
  2. They rolled the barrel down into the cellar.
  3. This wine was aged in a used bourbon barrel.

2. The long metal tube of a gun that the bullet travels through. (ствол (оружия))

  1. He looked straight down the barrel of the rifle.
  2. The barrel was still warm.
  3. Both barrels of the shotgun were empty.

3. A standard unit for measuring oil, equal to 42 gallons. (баррель (мера нефти))

  1. Crude oil closed at 78 dollars a barrel.
  2. The field produces thirty thousand barrels a day.
  3. A cold snap pushed the price per barrel higher.

round#

Pronunciation
/raʊnd/ · ROWND
Русский
угощение (по напитку каждому в компании); раунд, тур, этап; патрон; обход (врача, почтальона)
Plural
rounds
Countability
countable

Round has 4 distinct senses.

1. A drink bought for everyone in the group at the same time. (угощение (по напитку каждому в компании))

  1. This round is on me.
  2. They bought three rounds before dinner even arrived.
  3. It's your turn to get the next round.

2. One stage in a competition or in a series of events. (раунд, тур, этап)

  1. They were knocked out in the first round.
  2. I made it to the final round of interviews.
  3. The next round of talks starts in April.

3. A single bullet or shell for a gun. (патрон)

  1. He fired three rounds into the target.
  2. The magazine holds fifteen rounds.
  3. There was one round left in the chamber.

4. A regular series of visits made as part of a job. (обход (врача, почтальона))

  1. The doctor is on her morning rounds.
  2. The mail carrier finishes his round by two.
  3. Security makes rounds of the building every hour.

tab#

Pronunciation
/tæb/ · TAB
Русский
счёт (в баре или кафе); вкладка (в браузере); язычок, ярлычок, выступ
Plural
tabs
Countability
countable

Tab has 3 distinct senses.

1. A running bill for drinks that you settle when you leave. (счёт (в баре или кафе))

  1. Can I start a tab?
  2. He closed out his tab and left.
  3. Put it on my tab.

2. A page open in a web browser, shown as a small labeled strip along the top. (вкладка (в браузере))

  1. I had twenty tabs open and my laptop froze.
  2. Open that link in a new tab.
  3. Close the tab before you share your screen.

3. A small flap or strip sticking out of something, used to pull it or label it. (язычок, ярлычок, выступ)

  1. Pull the tab to open the box.
  2. Each folder has a tab with the client's name on it.
  3. The tab on the soda can broke off.

hangover#

Pronunciation
/ˈhæŋoʊvər/ · HANG-oh-vur
Русский
похмелье; пережиток, наследие прошлого
Plural
hangovers
Countability
countable

Hangover has 2 distinct senses.

1. The headache and sickness that follow drinking too much. (похмелье)

  1. He woke up with a terrible hangover.
  2. Water before bed helps with the hangover.
  3. Hangovers get worse as you get older.

2. Something left over from an earlier time that no longer fits. (пережиток, наследие прошлого)

  1. The rule is a hangover from the 1970s.
  2. That fee is a hangover from the old contract.
  3. Downtown is still working through the hangover of the building boom.
