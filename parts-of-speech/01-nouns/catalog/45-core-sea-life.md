# Core vocabulary: sea life

> The everyday nouns of sea life — pronunciation, Russian, plural, and three examples each.

[← The grammar of nouns](../README.md) &middot; [All groups](README.md)

Part of the **core-vocabulary tier**. Groups 1–23 cover the nouns that misbehave; this tier covers the nouns you simply need to know. Entries are deliberately lean, because a regular countable noun has no grammatical story to tell. A **Contrast** line appears only where the word hides a genuine trap.

Where a word in this field *does* misbehave, it lives in the grammar tier instead and is not repeated here.

### porpoise

**Pronunciation:** /ˈpɔːrpəs/ &middot; *POR-puhs*
**Русский:** морская свинья (китообразное)
**Plural:** *porpoises*
**Countability:** countable
**Contrast:** The ending is said like the word *purpose*, not 'poyz'.

A small sea mammal like a dolphin but shorter, with a blunt nose.

1. *Porpoises* are stockier than dolphins and have blunter faces.
2. We spotted a *porpoise* in the bay at dawn.
3. The crew pulled in the nets to avoid catching a *porpoise*.

**2. A boot with a blade or wheels for gliding over ice or pavement.** (конёк)

4. One *skate* was a size too small.
