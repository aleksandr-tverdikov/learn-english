Core vocabulary: the city#

The everyday nouns of the city — pronunciation, Russian, plural, and three examples each.

← The grammar of nouns · All groups

Part of the core-vocabulary tier. Groups 1–23 cover the nouns that misbehave; this tier covers the nouns you simply need to know. Entries are deliberately lean, because a regular countable noun has no grammatical story to tell. A Contrast line appears only where the word hides a genuine trap.

Where a word in this field does misbehave, it lives in the grammar tier instead and is not repeated here.

city#

Pronunciation
/ˈsɪti/ · SIH-tee
Русский
город (крупный)
Plural
cities
Countability
countable

A large and important town where many people live and work.

  1. She moved to the city right after college.
  2. Both cities have great public transportation.
  3. The whole city lost power for six hours.

block#

Pronunciation
/blɑːk/ · BLAHK
Русский
квартал (между двумя улицами); брусок, кусок (дерева, льда, камня); препятствие, затор; психологический блок; блок, партия (билетов, времени)
Plural
blocks
Countability
countable
Contrast
Americans measure walking distance in blocks; British English has no everyday equivalent.

Block has 4 distinct senses.

1. The stretch of a street between two cross streets, or the buildings on it. (квартал (между двумя улицами))

  1. The pharmacy is two blocks from here.
  2. They live on the same block as my sister.
  3. We walked around the block to cool off.

2. A solid piece of hard material with flat sides. (брусок, кусок (дерева, льда, камня))

  1. He carved the bird out of a block of wood.
  2. A block of ice sat melting in the cooler.
  3. The wall is built of concrete blocks.

3. Something that stops movement or progress. (препятствие, затор; психологический блок)

  1. She has had a mental block about math since third grade.
  2. There's a block somewhere in the drain.
  3. Fear can be a real block to learning.

4. A group or quantity of things handled together. (блок, партия (билетов, времени))

  1. The school bought a block of tickets for the show.
  2. I set aside a block of time every morning to write.
  3. A block of seats was reserved for the family.

borough#

Pronunciation
/ˈbɜːroʊ/ · BUR-oh
Русский
округ города, боро; район (административный)
Plural
boroughs
Countability
countable
Contrast
The 'gh' is silent: it rhymes with 'furrow', not with 'bore'.

Borough has 2 distinct senses.

1. One of the administrative divisions that a large city is split into. (округ города, боро)

  1. Queens is the largest borough by area.
  2. All five boroughs were under a flood warning.
  3. He's lived in the same borough his whole life.

2. A self-governing district of a large city or state. (район (административный))

  1. Brooklyn is the most populous borough.
  2. All five boroughs voted the same way.
  3. The borough runs its own sanitation department.

Pronunciation

Avenue has 2 distinct senses.

1. A wide city street, often lined with trees. (проспект, авеню)

  1. The parade goes straight down Fifth Avenue.
  2. Most of the avenues here run north to south.
  3. Her office is on a busy avenue near the park.

2. A possible way of doing something or making progress. (путь, способ (достижения цели))

  1. We've explored every avenue and come up empty.
  2. Selling online opened a new avenue for the shop.
  3. Appealing the ruling is her only remaining avenue.

Curb has 2 distinct senses.

1. The raised stone edge between a sidewalk and the road. (бордюр, поребрик)

  1. Leave the trash cans by the curb.
  2. The city repainted all the yellow curbs.

2. Something that limits or holds back an activity. (ограничение, сдерживающая мера)

  1. The city put a curb on short-term rentals.
  2. There is no real curb on what they can charge.
  3. New curbs on spending take effect in July.

track#

Pronunciation
/træk/ · TRAK
Русский
железнодорожный путь, рельсы; трек, дорожка (запись); беговая дорожка, трек; след, следы
Plural
tracks
Countability
countable

Track has 4 distinct senses.

1. The pair of metal rails a train runs on. (железнодорожный путь, рельсы)

  1. Never walk on the tracks.
  2. A freight train blocked the track for an hour.
  3. The old tracks were torn up and turned into a trail.

2. A single song or piece of music on an album. (трек, дорожка (запись))

  1. The third track is my favorite.
  2. The album has twelve tracks.
  3. They released one track early as a single.

3. A course, usually oval, for running or racing. (беговая дорожка, трек)

  1. She runs four miles on the track every morning.
  2. The horses came around the far turn of the track.
  3. Our school just resurfaced the track.

4. A line of marks left on the ground by an animal, person, or vehicle. (след, следы)

  1. There were deer tracks in the snow.
  2. Muddy tracks led from the door to the garage.
  3. The hunter followed the bear's tracks for a mile.

Pipeline has 2 distinct senses.

  1. The pipeline runs under the whole valley.

  2. The company built a pipeline of young engineers straight out of college.
  3. There's a long pipeline of orders still waiting to ship.
