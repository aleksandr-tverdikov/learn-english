Core vocabulary: meat, fish, and dairy#

The everyday nouns of meat, fish, and dairy — pronunciation, Russian, plural, and three examples each.

← The grammar of nouns · All groups

Part of the core-vocabulary tier. Groups 1–23 cover the nouns that misbehave; this tier covers the nouns you simply need to know. Entries are deliberately lean, because a regular countable noun has no grammatical story to tell. A Contrast line appears only where the word hides a genuine trap.

Where a word in this field does misbehave, it lives in the grammar tier instead and is not repeated here.

lamb#

Pronunciation
/læm/ · LAM
Русский
баранина (мясо ягнёнка); ягнёнок
Plural
lambs
Countability
both — countable and uncountable with different meanings
Contrast
The b is silent — LAM, exactly like the first half of "lamp" without the p.

A young sheep, or the meat that comes from one.

  1. We had roast lamb with mint sauce for Easter.
  2. The lambs were out in the field by March.
  3. Lamb has a stronger flavor than beef.

poultry#

Pronunciation
/ˈpoʊltri/ · POHL-tree
Русский
домашняя птица (как мясо); домашняя птица (мясо и живность)
Countability
uncountable
Contrast
Uncountable: poultry is, never a poultry or poultries.

Poultry has 2 distinct senses.

1. Chicken, turkey, duck and other farm birds raised for meat or eggs. (домашняя птица (как мясо))

  1. Wash your hands after handling raw poultry.
  2. The poultry section is at the back of the store.
  3. Prices for poultry stayed flat all year.

2. Birds such as chickens and turkeys kept for eggs and meat, or their meat. (домашняя птица (мясо и живность))

  1. The farm sells poultry and eggs at the market.
  2. Poultry is cheaper than beef right now.
  3. He does not eat red meat, only fish and poultry.

chop#

Pronunciation
/tʃɑːp/ · CHAHP
Русский
отбивная (на косточке); рубящий удар; удар ребром ладони
Plural
chops
Countability
countable

Chop has 2 distinct senses.

1. A thick slice of meat, usually pork or lamb, cut with the bone still in. (отбивная (на косточке))

  1. She pan-fried two pork chops in butter.
  2. The lamb chop was cooked perfectly.
  3. Chops dry out fast, so watch the heat.

2. A short, sharp downward blow with the side of the hand or with an axe. (рубящий удар; удар ребром ладони)

  1. He knocked the board apart with one chop.
  2. A karate chop to the neck ended the fight.
  3. Two quick chops of the axe split the kindling.

flank#

Pronunciation
/flæŋk/ · FLANGK
Русский
пашина (мясной отруб); бок (тела); фланг
Plural
flanks
Countability
countable, and uncountable as food

Flank has 3 distinct senses.

1. A lean cut of beef from the belly area below the ribs. (пашина (мясной отруб))

  1. Flank steak is best sliced against the grain.
  2. She marinated the flank for two hours.
  3. Grill the flank hot and fast.

2. The side of a person's or animal's body between the ribs and the hip. (бок (тела))

  1. The horse's flanks were dark with sweat.
  2. He felt a sharp pain in his left flank.
  3. She patted the cow's flank and stepped back.

3. The far left or right side of an army, a team, or a formation. (фланг)

  1. The enemy attacked on the left flank.
  2. Their defense is weak on both flanks.
  3. He sent riders out to protect the flank.
