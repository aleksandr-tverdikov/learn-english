Core vocabulary: work and the office#

The everyday nouns of work and the office — pronunciation, Russian, plural, and three examples each.

← The grammar of nouns · All groups

Part of the core-vocabulary tier. Groups 1–23 cover the nouns that misbehave; this tier covers the nouns you simply need to know. Entries are deliberately lean, because a regular countable noun has no grammatical story to tell. A Contrast line appears only where the word hides a genuine trap.

Where a word in this field does misbehave, it lives in the grammar tier instead and is not repeated here.

  1. She manages a team of six direct reports.
  2. Every new manager starts with two or three reports.
  3. He has more reports than anyone else at his level.

form#

Pronunciation
/fɔːrm/ · FORM
Русский
бланк, анкета; форма, очертания; вид, разновидность; форма (состояние, уровень игры)
Plural
forms
Countability
countable

Form has 4 distinct senses.

1. A printed sheet with blank spaces to be filled in. (бланк, анкета)

  1. Fill out this form and bring it back.
  2. The form asks for your Social Security number.
  3. There were four forms to sign on my first day.

2. The shape or outward appearance of something. (форма, очертания)

  1. A dark form moved behind the curtain.
  2. The carving keeps the rough form of a bird.
  3. We could barely make out the forms of the mountains in the fog.

3. A type or variety of something. (вид, разновидность)

  1. Swimming is the best form of exercise for her knees.
  2. Cash is still an accepted form of payment here.
  3. The disease takes several different forms.

4. The level at which a person or team is performing at the moment. (форма (состояние, уровень игры))

  1. The team has been in great form since the break.
  2. After his injury he struggled to get back to form.
  3. The comedian was in fine form last night.

survey#

Pronunciation
/ˈsɜːrveɪ/ · SUR-vay
Русский
опрос, анкетирование; межевание, геодезическая съёмка; обзор, общий осмотр
Plural
surveys
Countability
countable
Contrast
Stress moves: the noun is SUR-vay, the verb to survey is sur-VAY.

Survey has 3 distinct senses.

1. A set of questions asked of many people to collect opinions. (опрос, анкетирование)

  1. HR sent out an employee survey.
  2. The survey takes about five minutes.
  3. Nobody fills out these surveys.

2. A careful measurement of a piece of land and its exact boundaries. (межевание, геодезическая съёмка)

  1. The bank required a survey before we closed on the house.
  2. The survey showed the fence was two feet onto the neighbor's lot.
  3. Crews finished the survey of the highway route last week.

3. A general look at or review of a whole subject. (обзор, общий осмотр)

  1. The book is a survey of American art since 1945.
  2. He took a quick survey of the damage after the storm.
  3. Freshmen usually start with a survey course.
