Core vocabulary: measurement#

measurement#

Pronunciation
/ˈmɛʒərmənt/ · MEZH-ur-muhnt
Русский
измерение, замер
Plural
measurements
Countability
countable
Contrast
The s is a zh sound, as in pleasure: MEZH-ur-muhnt.

Measurement has 2 distinct senses.

1. A figure showing the size, length, or amount of something. (измерение, замер)

  1. Take a measurement before you cut the board.
  2. The tailor wrote down all my measurements.
  3. That measurement looks off by half an inch.

2. A number that you get by measuring something. (измерение, замер)

  1. Take three measurements and average them.
  2. One measurement was clearly wrong.
  3. Her measurements matched the published values.

Measure has 2 distinct senses.

1. A unit or standard used to state size or amount. (мера, единица измерения)

  1. A cup is a standard measure in American recipes.
  2. The inch is an old measure of length.
  3. The rest of the world uses metric measures.

2. A short group of beats between two vertical lines in written music. (такт)

  1. The solo starts in the fourth measure.
  2. Count four measures of rest, then come in.
  3. Play the last two measures again.

unit#

Pronunciation
/ˈjuːnɪt/ · YOO-nit
Русский

Plural

Countability
countable
Contrast
It starts with a y sound, so it takes a, not an: a unit.

Unit has 4 distinct senses.

1. A fixed amount used as a standard for measuring. (единица измерения)

  1. The meter is the basic unit of length in the metric system.
  2. Convert both numbers to the same units before you add them.
  3. Electricity is billed by the unit.

2. A group of people forming one part of a larger organization. (подразделение, отряд, отдел)

  1. His unit was stationed in Germany for two years.
  2. She works in the department's research unit.
  3. Both units report to the same commander.

3. A single apartment or home inside a larger building or complex. (квартира (в жилом комплексе))

  1. The building has forty-eight units.
  2. Their unit is on the third floor, facing the park.
  3. Two units are still available for rent.

4. A separate machine or piece of equipment that does one job. (агрегат, блок, установка)

  1. The air conditioning unit sits on the roof.
  2. A new heating unit runs a few thousand dollars.
  3. The unit quit in the middle of the heat wave.

  1. Her uncle is a terrible bore at parties.

  1. The engine has a four-inch bore.
