Core vocabulary: religion and belief#

The everyday nouns of religion and belief — pronunciation, Russian, plural, and three examples each.

← The grammar of nouns · All groups

Part of the core-vocabulary tier. Groups 1–23 cover the nouns that misbehave; this tier covers the nouns you simply need to know. Entries are deliberately lean, because a regular countable noun has no grammatical story to tell. A Contrast line appears only where the word hides a genuine trap.

Where a word in this field does misbehave, it lives in the grammar tier instead and is not repeated here.

church#

Pronunciation
/tʃɜːrtʃ/ · CHURCH
Русский
церковь, храм; богослужение, служба; церковь (организация, конфессия)
Plural
churches
Countability
countable

Church has 3 distinct senses.

1. A building where Christians meet to worship. (церковь, храм)

  1. There's a little white church at the end of the road.
  2. We go to church every Sunday morning.
  3. Half the churches in town run a food pantry.

2. A Christian service of worship. (богослужение, служба)

  1. Church starts at ten on Sunday.
  2. We ran into them right after church.
  3. She reads to the kids during church.

3. A branch of the Christian religion, or the Christian religion as an organized body. (церковь (организация, конфессия))

  1. The church has spoken out against the new law.
  2. He was raised in the Baptist church.
  3. Churches across the country are losing members.

  1. Hard work and plain talk were his whole creed.
  2. The company printed its creed on a single page.
  3. She stuck to her creed of never lending money to friends.

/ˈmɪrəkəl/ · MIH-ruh-kuhl
Русский
чудо; чудо (нечто удивительное)
Plural
miracles
Countability
countable

Miracle has 2 distinct senses.

1. An event that cannot be explained by natural law, seen as the work of God. (чудо)

  1. The doctors called her recovery a miracle.
  2. It's a miracle nobody was hurt.
  3. The gospel describes several miracles.

2. Something wonderful and lucky that seemed impossible. (чудо (нечто удивительное))

  1. It's a miracle nobody was hurt in that crash.
  2. The country's economic miracle lasted twenty years.
  3. Getting four kids out the door on time was a small miracle.
