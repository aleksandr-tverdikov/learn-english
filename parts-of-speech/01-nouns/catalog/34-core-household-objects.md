# Core vocabulary: household objects

> The everyday nouns of household objects — pronunciation, Russian, plural, and three examples each.

[← The grammar of nouns](../README.md) &middot; [All groups](README.md)

Part of the **core-vocabulary tier**. Groups 1–23 cover the nouns that misbehave; this tier covers the nouns you simply need to know. Entries are deliberately lean, because a regular countable noun has no grammatical story to tell. A **Contrast** line appears only where the word hides a genuine trap.

Where a word in this field *does* misbehave, it lives in the grammar tier instead and is not repeated here.

---
### washing machine

**Pronunciation:** /ˈwɑːʃɪŋ məˌʃiːn/ &middot; *WAH-shing muh-SHEEN*
**Русский:** стиральная машина
**Plural:** *washing machines*
**Countability:** countable

An appliance that washes clothes.

1. The *washing machine* stopped in the middle of a cycle.
2. He threw everything into the *washing machine* at once.
3. There are four *washing machines* in the basement.

**Pronunciation:** /ˈdraɪər/ &middot; *DRY-ur*
**Русский:** сушильная машина
