Core vocabulary: furniture#

The everyday nouns of furniture — pronunciation, Russian, plural, and three examples each.

← The grammar of nouns · All groups

Part of the core-vocabulary tier. Groups 1–23 cover the nouns that misbehave; this tier covers the nouns you simply need to know. Entries are deliberately lean, because a regular countable noun has no grammatical story to tell. A Contrast line appears only where the word hides a genuine trap.

Where a word in this field does misbehave, it lives in the grammar tier instead and is not repeated here.

chair#

Pronunciation
/tʃɛr/ · CHAIR
Русский
стул; кресло; председатель; заведующий кафедрой
Plural
chairs
Countability
countable

Chair has 2 distinct senses.

1. A seat for one person, usually with a back and four legs. (стул; кресло)

  1. Pull up a chair and sit with us.
  2. We need two more chairs for the dinner table.
  3. He tipped his chair back and put his feet up.

2. The person who leads a meeting, a committee, or a university department. (председатель; заведующий кафедрой)

  1. The chair called the meeting to order.
  2. She was elected chair of the school board.
  3. He is the chair of the biology department.

  1. The report includes a table of monthly sales.
  2. Check the table on page 12 for the exact figures.
  3. She turned the raw data into a simple table.

  1. He clamped the board to the bench and started sanding.
  2. Her tools were laid out neatly on the bench.
  3. The engine sat on a bench at the back of the shop.

seat#

Pronunciation
/siːt/ · SEET
Русский

Plural

Countability
countable

Seat has 3 distinct senses.

1. The part of a chair you sit on, or a place to sit. (сиденье; место)

  1. The seat on that chair is starting to sag.
  2. Save me a seat if you get there first.
  3. All the good seats were gone by seven.

2. A place on an elected body or a board, held by one member. (место (в парламенте, совете), мандат)

  1. She won a seat in the state senate.
  2. The party lost twelve seats in the last election.
  3. He holds a seat on the school board.

3. The place where a government, court, or organization is based. (местопребывание, центр (власти))

  1. Washington is the seat of the federal government.
  2. The county seat is thirty miles north of here.
  3. For centuries the town was the seat of the local bishop.
