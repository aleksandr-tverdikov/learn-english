# Core vocabulary: camping and the outdoors

> The everyday nouns of camping and the outdoors — pronunciation, Russian, plural, and three examples each.

[← The grammar of nouns](../README.md) &middot; [All groups](README.md)

Part of the **core-vocabulary tier**. Groups 1–23 cover the nouns that misbehave; this tier covers the nouns you simply need to know. Entries are deliberately lean, because a regular countable noun has no grammatical story to tell. A **Contrast** line appears only where the word hides a genuine trap.

Where a word in this field *does* misbehave, it lives in the grammar tier instead and is not repeated here.

### campground

**Pronunciation:** /ˈkæmpɡraʊnd/ &middot; *KAMP-grownd*
**Русский:** кемпинг, оборудованная площадка для палаток
**Plural:** *campgrounds*
**Countability:** countable

An area set aside for people to camp, usually with numbered sites and restrooms.

1. The *campground* fills up fast on holiday weekends.
2. There are two *campgrounds* on the north side of the lake.
3. We pulled into the *campground* just before dark.

### water filter

**Pronunciation:** /ˈwɔːtər ˌfɪltər/ &middot; *WAW-tur-fil-tur*
**Русский:** фильтр для воды
**Plural:** *water filters*
**Countability:** countable

Water filter has 2 distinct senses.

**1. A device that makes stream or lake water safe to drink.** (фильтр для воды)

1. Run it through the *water filter* first.
2. Backpacking *water filters* have gotten a lot lighter.
3. The *water filter* clogged up with silt.

**2. A device that removes dirt, taste, or chemicals from water.** (фильтр для воды)

4. The *water filter* on the fridge needs changing.
5. A *water filter* under the sink handles the whole kitchen.
6. *Water filters* for that model are hard to find.

**2. Sudden dismissal from a job, or the cancellation of a program.** (увольнение; закрытие (проекта))
