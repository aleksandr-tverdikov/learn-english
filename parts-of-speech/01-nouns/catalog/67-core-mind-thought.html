Core vocabulary: mind and thought#

The everyday nouns of mind and thought — pronunciation, Russian, plural, and three examples each.

← The grammar of nouns · All groups

Part of the core-vocabulary tier. Groups 1–23 cover the nouns that misbehave; this tier covers the nouns you simply need to know. Entries are deliberately lean, because a regular countable noun has no grammatical story to tell. A Contrast line appears only where the word hides a genuine trap.

Where a word in this field does misbehave, it lives in the grammar tier instead and is not repeated here.

mind#

Pronunciation
/maɪnd/ · MYND
Русский
разум, ум; ум, светлая голова (о человеке); мнение, намерение
Plural
minds
Countability
countable

Mind has 3 distinct senses.

1. The part of a person that thinks, feels, and remembers. (разум, ум)

  1. Something was on her mind all afternoon.
  2. He has one of the sharpest minds in the department.
  3. I've changed my mind about the job offer.

2. A person of great intelligence. (ум, светлая голова (о человеке))

  1. She was one of the finest minds of her generation.
  2. The lab attracts the best young minds in the field.
  3. It took a great mind to see the pattern at all.

3. A person's opinion or intention about something. (мнение, намерение)

  1. She changed her mind about going.
  2. He always speaks his mind at meetings.
  3. We were of the same mind on the budget.

opinion#

Pronunciation
/əˈpɪnjən/ · uh-PIN-yuhn
Русский

Plural

Countability
countable

Opinion has 3 distinct senses.

1. What a person thinks or believes about something. (мнение, взгляд)

  1. In my opinion, the second draft is much better.
  2. People have strong opinions about the new law.
  3. The doctor wants a second opinion before operating.

2. A judge's written explanation of a court's decision. (решение суда с обоснованием, заключение суда)

  1. The court's opinion ran to ninety pages.
  2. Two justices wrote dissenting opinions.
  3. The opinion did not settle the question for good.

3. Professional advice from an expert, especially a doctor. (заключение специалиста, мнение врача)

  1. Get a second opinion before you agree to surgery.
  2. The insurance company wants an opinion from its own doctor.
  3. Her lawyer gave a written opinion on the contract.

sense#

Pronunciation
/sɛns/ · SENS
Русский

Plural
senses
Countability
both — countable and uncountable with different meanings

Sense has 3 distinct senses.

1. One of the body's powers of perception, or practical good judgment. (чувство (ощущение); смысл, здравый смысл)

  1. A dog's sense of smell is remarkable.
  2. The cold water woke up all five of his senses.
  3. Have the sense to wear a coat when it's ten degrees out.

2. A feeling or awareness of something. (чувство, ощущение (чего-л.))

  1. I felt a huge sense of relief when the call finally came.
  2. He has no sense of direction at all.
  3. There's a growing sense that nothing will change.

3. One of the meanings of a word or phrase. (значение (слова))

  1. The word has at least four senses in this dictionary.
  2. In what sense are you using the word free?
  3. He meant it in the older sense of the word.

Conclusion has 2 distinct senses.

  1. I came to the conclusion that he was lying.

  1. His assumption of command came at a difficult moment.
  2. The assumption of power by the military drew protests.
  3. Assumption of the seller's mortgage saved the buyers thousands.
