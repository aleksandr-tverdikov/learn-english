# Core vocabulary: shapes and colors

> The everyday nouns of shapes and colors — pronunciation, Russian, plural, and three examples each.

[← The grammar of nouns](../README.md) &middot; [All groups](README.md)

Part of the **core-vocabulary tier**. Groups 1–23 cover the nouns that misbehave; this tier covers the nouns you simply need to know. Entries are deliberately lean, because a regular countable noun has no grammatical story to tell. A **Contrast** line appears only where the word hides a genuine trap.

Where a word in this field *does* misbehave, it lives in the grammar tier instead and is not repeated here.

### shape

**Pronunciation:** /ʃeɪp/ &middot; *SHAYP*
**Русский:** форма, очертание; состояние, форма (физическая)
**Plural:** *shapes*
**Countability:** countable

Shape has 2 distinct senses.

**1. The outer form of something, seen as an outline.** (форма, очертание)

1. The cookies were cut in the *shape* of stars.
2. Draw three different *shapes* on the board.
3. That cloud has a funny *shape*.

**2. The condition that a person or thing is in.** (состояние, форма (физическая))

4. He's in good *shape* for a man of seventy.
5. The car is in rough *shape* but it still runs.
6. The roof is in better *shape* than we expected.
