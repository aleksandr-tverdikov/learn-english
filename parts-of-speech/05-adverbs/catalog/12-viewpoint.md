# Viewpoint and domain adverbs

> From this point of view — *financially*, *technically*, *legally*, *personally*, *ideally*, *strictly speaking*.

[← The grammar of adverbs](../README.md) &middot; [All groups](README.md)

Viewpoint adverbs narrow the frame in which a statement is true: *Financially, it makes sense* claims nothing about whether it makes sense in any other way. They usually open the sentence with a comma. The pattern is productive — almost any *-al* adjective can do it — so this group lists the frequent ones and the ones whose viewpoint sense differs from their manner sense (*technically*, *practically*, *personally*).

---
### financially

**Pronunciation:** /fəˈnænʃəli/ &middot; *fuh-NAN-shuh-lee*
**Русский:** финансово, в финансовом отношении
**Type:** viewpoint; also manner — in terms of money
**Position:** any position — front with a comma as a viewpoint (*Financially, it works*); before an adjective (*financially stable*); end after a verb (*support them financially*)
**Comparison:** not gradable
**Modifies:** whole clauses, adjectives and participles, verbs
**Form:** -ly from *financial*; the first syllable is also said *fy-*
**Contrast:** Russian *материально помогать* is *help financially*, not *materially* — English *materially* means significantly (*it did not materially change the result*).

Financially has 2 distinct senses.

**1. From the point of view of money; when money is considered.** (с финансовой точки зрения, в финансовом плане)

1. *Financially*, the move to Texas made a lot of sense.
2. The plan looks good *financially*, but it will take years.
3. *Financially*, we're in much better shape than last year.

**2. In a way that concerns money — having it, earning it, or giving it.** (материально, финансово)

4. My parents still support me *financially*.
5. She wants to be *financially* independent by thirty.
6. A lot of families are struggling *financially* right now.

### politically

**Pronunciation:** /pəˈlɪtɪkli/ &middot; *puh-LIT-ik-lee*
**Русский:** политически, с политической точки зрения
**Type:** viewpoint; also manner — in terms of politics
**Position:** any position — front with a comma as a viewpoint; before an adjective or participle (*politically active*)
**Comparison:** not gradable
**Modifies:** whole clauses, adjectives and participles, verbs
**Form:** -ly from *political*; the *-al-* is usually not pronounced: *puh-LIT-ik-lee*
**Contrast:** *Politically correct* is a fixed phrase: avoiding words that could offend a group, often said with irony. It does not mean correct from a political point of view. Its opposite is *politically incorrect*.

Politically has 2 distinct senses.

**1. From a political point of view; when politics or public opinion is considered.** (с политической точки зрения)

1. *Politically*, raising taxes right before an election would be suicide.
2. The plan makes sense on paper, but *politically* it's a nonstarter.
3. *Politically*, the governor can't afford to lose the suburbs.

**2. In a way connected with politics, government, or political parties.** (политически)

4. My grandmother was *politically* active her whole life.
5. Police say the attack was *politically* motivated.
6. It's one of the most *politically* divided states in the country.

### technically

**Pronunciation:** /ˈtɛknɪkli/ &middot; *TEK-nik-lee*
**Русский:** формально, строго говоря; технически
**Type:** viewpoint; also manner
**Position:** any position — front with a comma for 'strictly speaking' (*Technically, …*); mid (*it's technically legal*); before an adjective (*technically difficult*)
**Comparison:** not gradable
**Modifies:** whole clauses, verbs, adjectives
**Form:** -ly from *technical*; the *-al-* is usually not pronounced: *TEK-nik-lee*
**Contrast:** The most common American sense has nothing to do with technology: *Technically, he's still my boss* means формально — the rules say so, though real life may differ, and a *but* often follows. Russian *технически* covers only sense 2. Don't confuse it with *practically* or *virtually*, which mean almost.

Technically has 2 distinct senses.

**1. According to the exact rules or the strict meaning of words, even if not in reality.** (формально, строго говоря)

1. *Technically*, you're still married until the judge signs the papers.
2. It's *technically* legal, but it's a terrible idea.
3. *Technically*, the store closes at nine, but they let people finish shopping.

**2. In terms of technology or technique — the equipment and methods involved, or the skill shown in an art, sport, or craft.** (технически, с технической точки зрения, в плане техники)

4. *Technically*, the new phone is a big step forward.
5. The bridge was *technically* difficult to build.
6. She's a *technically* brilliant pianist, but her playing feels cold.

### legally

**Pronunciation:** /ˈliːɡəli/ &middot; *LEE-guh-lee*
**Русский:** юридически; законно
**Type:** viewpoint; also manner
**Position:** any position — front with a comma as a viewpoint; before an adjective (*legally binding*); end after a verb (*buy it legally*)
**Comparison:** not gradable
**Modifies:** whole clauses, adjectives and participles, verbs
**Form:** -ly from *legal*
**Contrast:** *Legally blind* is an American legal category — sight so poor (20/200 or worse with glasses) that a person qualifies for benefits — not total blindness. Where Russian says *юридически*, English says *legally*: *legally binding* is юридически обязывающий.

Legally has 2 distinct senses.

**1. From the point of view of the law; as far as the law is concerned.** (юридически, с юридической точки зрения)

1. *Legally*, the landlord has to return your deposit within 30 days.
2. Once you sign it, the contract is *legally* binding.
3. Parents are *legally* responsible for getting their kids to school.

**2. In a way that is allowed by law.** (законно, легально)

4. You can't *legally* park here after 6 p.m.
5. You have to be 21 to buy alcohol *legally*.
6. He came into the country *legally*.

### medically

**Pronunciation:** /ˈmɛdɪkli/ &middot; *MED-ik-lee*
**Русский:** с медицинской точки зрения; медикаментозно
**Type:** viewpoint; also manner
**Position:** any position — front with a comma as a viewpoint; before an adjective or participle (*medically necessary*); end after a verb (*treated medically*)
**Comparison:** not gradable
**Modifies:** whole clauses, adjectives and participles, verbs
**Form:** -ly from *medical*; the *-al-* is usually not pronounced: *MED-ik-lee*
**Contrast:** *Medically* is about medicine as a field; *medicinally* means as a medicine (*herbs used medicinally*). In American health insurance, *medically necessary* is a fixed term for the care a plan has to pay for.

Medically has 2 distinct senses.

**1. From a medical point of view; in terms of health and medicine.** (с медицинской точки зрения, медицински)

1. *Medically*, there's nothing wrong with him — the pain may be stress.
2. Insurance only covers the surgery if it's *medically* necessary.
3. She was *medically* cleared to play on Saturday.

**2. By means of medical treatment, especially drugs rather than surgery.** (медикаментозно, с помощью медицины)

4. Most cases can be managed *medically* without an operation.
5. The patient was put in a *medically* induced coma.

### personally

**Pronunciation:** /ˈpɜːrsənəli/ &middot; *PUR-suh-nuh-lee*
**Русский:** лично; по-моему
**Type:** viewpoint; also manner
**Position:** any position — front with a comma for an opinion (*Personally, I…*); mid before the verb for 'yourself' (*she personally called*); end after *take it* or *know someone*
**Comparison:** not gradable — except *take it very personally*
**Modifies:** whole clauses, verbs, adjectives (*personally responsible*)
**Form:** -ly from *personal*; double *l*
**Contrast:** *Personally* is not *in person*: *I met him in person* means face to face; *I know him personally* means we are acquainted, not just by name. *Take it personally* is the set phrase for принимать на свой счёт. The opinion sense almost always goes with *I* or *we*: *Personally, I think…*.

Personally has 4 distinct senses.

**1. Used to show that you are giving your own opinion.** (лично я, по-моему)

1. *Personally*, I think the first movie was better.
2. I don't care for sushi *personally*, but the kids love it.
3. *Personally*, I'd wait until the price drops.

**2. By yourself, not through someone else.** (лично, сам)

4. The mayor *personally* called to apologize.
5. I'll deliver the check *personally*.
6. You'll be *personally* responsible for the equipment.

**3. As a person — in terms of character or a private relationship, not a professional or public role.** (лично, как человек)

7. I don't know the senator *personally*, only from the news.
8. The two candidates dislike each other *personally*, not just politically.

**4. As an attack on or criticism of yourself, especially in *take something personally*.** (на свой счёт)

9. Don't take it *personally* — he's rude to everyone.
10. She took the rejection very *personally*.

### theoretically

**Pronunciation:** /ˌθiːəˈrɛtɪkli/ &middot; *thee-uh-RET-ik-lee*
**Русский:** теоретически, в теории
**Type:** viewpoint
**Position:** any position — front with a comma; mid (*you could theoretically*); before an adjective (*it's theoretically possible*)
**Comparison:** not gradable
**Modifies:** whole clauses, adjectives, verbs
**Form:** -ly from *theoretical*; the *-al-* is usually not pronounced
**Contrast:** Like *technically*, sense 1 hints that reality is different: *Theoretically, it's possible* usually means don't count on it. The natural partner is *in practice*: *Theoretically…, but in practice…*.

Theoretically has 2 distinct senses.

**1. In theory — according to what should happen, though it may not happen in practice.** (теоретически, в теории)

1. *Theoretically*, the drive takes three hours, but not with Friday traffic.
2. It's *theoretically* possible to see the northern lights from Chicago.
3. *Theoretically*, anyone can apply for the job.

**2. In terms of theory; from the standpoint of an academic or scientific theory.** (в теоретическом плане)

4. The paper is *theoretically* interesting but has very little data.
5. Her research is *theoretically* grounded in behavioral economics.

### statistically

**Pronunciation:** /stəˈtɪstɪkli/ &middot; *stuh-TIS-tik-lee*
**Русский:** статистически, по статистике
**Type:** viewpoint; also manner
**Position:** any position — front with a comma as a viewpoint; before an adjective (*statistically significant*)
**Comparison:** not gradable
**Modifies:** whole clauses, adjectives and participles
**Form:** -ly from *statistical*; the *-al-* is usually not pronounced
**Contrast:** *Statistically significant* is a technical term — unlikely to be the result of chance. It is not a synonym of large or important: a tiny difference can be statistically significant.

Statistically has 2 distinct senses.

**1. According to statistics; when you look at the numbers.** (по статистике, статистически)

1. *Statistically*, flying is much safer than driving.
2. *Statistically*, most new restaurants close within a few years.
3. Young men are *statistically* more likely to get into car accidents.

**2. By the methods of statistics.** (статистически)

4. The difference between the two groups was not *statistically* significant.
5. The survey results were analyzed *statistically*.

### historically

**Pronunciation:** /hɪˈstɔːrɪkli/ &middot; *hih-STOR-ik-lee*
**Русский:** исторически, традиционно
**Type:** viewpoint; also time
**Position:** any position — front with a comma for 'in the past'; mid (*have historically outperformed*); before an adjective (*historically accurate*)
**Comparison:** not gradable
**Modifies:** whole clauses, verbs, adjectives
**Form:** -ly from both *historic* and *historical*; the *-al-* is usually not pronounced
**Contrast:** One adverb serves two adjectives: *historic* (important in history) and *historical* (about history), so both *historically important* and *historically accurate*. In sense 1 the tense shows whether the pattern goes on: present perfect if it still does (*Historically, the state has voted Democratic*), past simple if it has ended (*Historically, women were paid less*). In American English the article is *a*, not *an*: *a historically important building*.

Historically has 2 distinct senses.

**1. In the past, over a long period; traditionally; also compared with the past (*historically low*).** (исторически, традиционно, издавна)

1. *Historically*, this neighborhood has been home to Italian immigrants.
2. Mortgage rates hit *historically* low levels during the pandemic.
3. *Historically*, women were paid less for the same work.

**2. In terms of history; from the point of view of a historian.** (исторически, с исторической точки зрения)

4. The movie isn't very *historically* accurate.
5. It's a *historically* important building — the treaty was signed here.
6. *Historically*, the Battle of Gettysburg was the turning point of the Civil War.

### culturally

**Pronunciation:** /ˈkʌltʃərəli/ &middot; *KUHL-chur-uh-lee*
**Русский:** в культурном отношении
**Type:** viewpoint
**Position:** any position — front with a comma; before an adjective (*culturally diverse*)
**Comparison:** not gradable
**Modifies:** whole clauses, adjectives
**Form:** -ly from *cultural*; double *l*
**Contrast:** False friend: Russian *культурно* (вести себя культурно) means politely, with good manners — English *politely*, *decently*. *Culturally* only means in terms of culture.

Culturally has 2 distinct senses.

**1. In terms of a society's customs, traditions, and beliefs.** (в культурном отношении, с точки зрения культуры)

1. *Culturally*, the two countries have a lot in common.
2. Our school is *culturally* diverse — the kids speak twenty languages at home.
3. In some places it isn't *culturally* acceptable to refuse food from a host.

**2. In terms of the arts — music, theater, museums, and literature.** (в плане культурной жизни)

4. *Culturally*, Chicago has as much to offer as New York.
5. The town is small but *culturally* rich, with two theaters and a jazz club.

### economically

**Pronunciation:** /ˌɛkəˈnɑːmɪkli/ &middot; *ek-uh-NAHM-ik-lee*
**Русский:** экономически; экономно
**Type:** viewpoint; also manner
**Position:** any position — front with a comma as a viewpoint; before an adjective (*economically viable*); end after a verb for 'thriftily' (*live economically*)
**Comparison:** more/most — only in the 'thriftily' sense (*more economically*); not gradable as a viewpoint
**Modifies:** whole clauses, adjectives and participles, verbs
**Form:** -ly from both *economic* (about the economy) and *economical* (saving money); also said *ee-kuh-NAHM-ik-lee*
**Contrast:** The two senses belong to two adjectives: *economic growth* (экономический) but *an economical car* (экономичный). The adverb is the same word, so context decides: *economically weak* is экономически слабый; *use it economically* is экономно.

Economically has 2 distinct senses.

**1. In terms of the economy, trade, or wealth.** (экономически, в экономическом отношении)

1. *Economically*, the town never recovered after the plant closed.
2. The project isn't *economically* viable without federal money.
3. Kids from *economically* disadvantaged families get free lunch.

**2. In a way that uses little money, time, or material, without waste.** (экономно, бережливо)

4. We're trying to live more *economically* until I find a new job.
5. Use the paint *economically* — it's all we have.
6. She writes *economically*, never wasting a word.

### logistically

**Pronunciation:** /ləˈdʒɪstɪkli/ &middot; *luh-JIS-tik-lee*
**Русский:** с организационной точки зрения, логистически
**Type:** viewpoint
**Position:** any position — front with a comma; before an adjective (*logistically difficult*)
**Comparison:** not gradable
**Modifies:** whole clauses, adjectives
**Form:** -ly from *logistical*; the first syllable is also said *loh-*
**Contrast:** Not related to logic: *logically* is логично, по логике; *logistically* is about planning, transport, and supplies. *Logistically impossible* means it can't be organized, not that it makes no sense.

In terms of the practical planning and organization of people, transport, and supplies.

1. *Logistically*, getting 200 guests to an island is a nightmare.
2. The move was *logistically* complicated, with three trucks and two dogs.
3. It's a great idea, but *logistically* we can't pull it off by June.

### structurally

**Pronunciation:** /ˈstrʌktʃərəli/ &middot; *STRUHK-chur-uh-lee*
**Русский:** структурно; по конструкции
**Type:** viewpoint
**Position:** any position — front with a comma; before an adjective (*structurally sound*); end after a verb (*changed structurally*)
**Comparison:** not gradable
**Modifies:** whole clauses, adjectives, verbs
**Form:** -ly from *structural*; double *l*
**Contrast:** In engineering Russian, *конструктивно* (по конструкции) is *structurally* or *in design* — not *constructively*, which in English only means in a helpful way (*constructive criticism*). *Structurally sound* is the set phrase for a building that is safe and not about to fall down.

Structurally has 2 distinct senses.

**1. In terms of how a building, bridge, or object is built and holds together.** (по конструкции, в плане прочности)

1. The inspector said the house is *structurally* sound.
2. The bridge was *structurally* damaged in the earthquake.
3. *Structurally*, the old barn is in better shape than it looks.

**2. In terms of the way the parts of a system, text, or organization are arranged.** (структурно, по структуре)

4. *Structurally*, the novel moves between three timelines.
5. English and German are *structurally* similar.
6. The economy has changed *structurally* since the 1980s.

### scientifically

**Pronunciation:** /ˌsaɪənˈtɪfɪkli/ &middot; *sy-uhn-TIF-ik-lee*
**Русский:** научно, с научной точки зрения
**Type:** viewpoint; also manner
**Position:** any position — front with a comma as a viewpoint; before an adjective or participle (*scientifically proven*); end after a verb (*test it scientifically*)
**Comparison:** more/most — in the 'by scientific methods' sense (*more scientifically*); not gradable as a viewpoint
**Modifies:** whole clauses, adjectives and participles, verbs
**Form:** -ally from *scientific* (there is no *scientifical*); the ending is said *-ik-lee*

Scientifically has 2 distinct senses.

**1. According to science; from a scientific point of view.** (с научной точки зрения, научно)

1. *Scientifically*, there's no evidence that the diet works.
2. It's *scientifically* proven that smoking causes cancer.
3. The ad's claims aren't *scientifically* accurate.

**2. Using the careful, systematic methods of science.** (научно, по научной методике)

4. The drug has never been *scientifically* tested on children.
5. We need to approach the problem more *scientifically*, one variable at a time.

### morally

**Pronunciation:** /ˈmɔːrəli/ &middot; *MOR-uh-lee*
**Русский:** морально, с нравственной точки зрения
**Type:** viewpoint; also manner
**Position:** any position — front with a comma as a viewpoint; before an adjective (*morally wrong*); end after a verb (*act morally*)
**Comparison:** more/most — in the 'rightly' sense (*more morally*); not gradable as a viewpoint
**Modifies:** whole clauses, adjectives, verbs
**Form:** -ly from *moral*; double *l*
**Contrast:** False friend: Russian *морально* often means emotionally or mentally — *морально тяжело* is *emotionally draining*, *морально подготовиться* is *prepare mentally*, *морально устал* is *mentally exhausted*, and *морально устаревший* is *outdated* or *obsolete*. English *morally* is only about right and wrong; *moral support* is the one place the two languages match.

Morally has 2 distinct senses.

**1. From the point of view of right and wrong.** (морально, с нравственной точки зрения)

1. *Morally*, what he did was wrong, even if it was legal.
2. I think it's *morally* unacceptable to lie to a patient.
3. Is the company *morally* responsible for the spill?

**2. In a way that is right and good; by high moral standards.** (нравственно, порядочно)

4. She tried to live *morally* in a business full of cheats.
5. Most people believe they behave more *morally* than their neighbors.

### socially

**Pronunciation:** /ˈsoʊʃəli/ &middot; *SOH-shuh-lee*
**Русский:** социально; в компании; в общении
**Type:** viewpoint; also manner
**Position:** any position — front with a comma as a viewpoint; before an adjective (*socially acceptable*); end after a verb (*see someone socially*)
**Comparison:** not gradable
**Modifies:** whole clauses, adjectives, verbs
**Form:** -ly from *social*; double *l*
**Contrast:** *I drink socially* means only at parties and dinners with friends, not alone and not much — it has nothing to do with drinking in public. Don't confuse *socially* with *sociably* (in a friendly, outgoing way).

Socially has 3 distinct senses.

**1. In terms of society, social class, or what people in general accept.** (социально, в социальном отношении)

1. Smoking indoors is no longer *socially* acceptable.
2. *Socially*, smartphones have changed how teenagers date.
3. The program helps *socially* disadvantaged kids get into college.

**2. In situations where people meet for pleasure, outside work or duty.** (в компании, в неформальной обстановке, вне работы)

4. We work together, but we never see each other *socially*.
5. I only drink *socially*, maybe a beer at a barbecue.
6. It's hard to meet people *socially* after you move to a new city.

**3. In terms of relationships with other people and the ability to get along with them.** (в общении, в плане общения)

7. He's brilliant but *socially* awkward.
8. Our daughter did fine academically but struggled *socially* in middle school.

### emotionally

**Pronunciation:** /ɪˈmoʊʃənəli/ &middot; *ih-MOH-shuh-nuh-lee*
**Русский:** эмоционально, морально
**Type:** viewpoint; also manner
**Position:** any position — before an adjective (*emotionally exhausted*); front with a comma as a viewpoint; end after a verb (*speak emotionally*)
**Comparison:** more/most — in the 'with strong feeling' sense (*more emotionally*); not gradable as a viewpoint
**Modifies:** adjectives and participles, whole clauses, verbs
**Form:** -ly from *emotional*; double *l*
**Contrast:** This is where Russian *морально* usually belongs: *морально тяжело* is *emotionally hard* or *draining*, *моральная травма* is *emotional trauma*. Translating it as *morally* turns it into a question of right and wrong.

Emotionally has 2 distinct senses.

**1. In terms of feelings; with regard to a person's emotional state.** (эмоционально, морально, психологически)

1. After the funeral I was *emotionally* exhausted.
2. *Emotionally*, she's still not ready to date again.
3. Caring for a sick parent is *emotionally* draining.

**2. In a way that shows strong feelings.** (эмоционально, взволнованно)

4. He spoke *emotionally* about his late father.
5. Fans reacted *emotionally* when the team finally won the title.

### physically

**Pronunciation:** /ˈfɪzɪkli/ &middot; *FIZ-ik-lee*
**Русский:** физически
**Type:** viewpoint; also manner
**Position:** any position — before an adjective (*physically fit*); front with a comma as a viewpoint; mid before a verb (*physically attacked*)
**Comparison:** not gradable
**Modifies:** adjectives and participles, whole clauses, verbs
**Form:** -ly from *physical*; the *-al-* is usually not pronounced: *FIZ-ik-lee*
**Contrast:** In everyday English *physically* is about the body or the material world; for the school subject say *in terms of physics*. In casual American speech *I physically can't* is an exaggeration for I really can't.

Physically has 3 distinct senses.

**1. In terms of the body rather than the mind.** (физически)

1. You need to be *physically* fit to join the fire department.
2. *Physically*, he's fine — it's his mental health we're worried about.
3. Construction work is *physically* demanding.

**2. In terms of material things or actual presence, as opposed to online, on paper, or in the mind.** (физически, реально, фактически)

4. The company is registered in Delaware but *physically* located in Denver.
5. Students must be *physically* present to take the exam.

**3. According to the laws of nature; in reality at all.** (физически (невозможно), никак)

6. It's *physically* impossible to be in two places at once.
7. There's no way — I *physically* can't finish this by Friday.

### mentally

**Pronunciation:** /ˈmɛntəli/ &middot; *MEN-tuh-lee*
**Русский:** психически, умственно; мысленно
**Type:** viewpoint; also manner
**Position:** any position — before an adjective (*mentally ill*); front with a comma as a viewpoint; mid before a verb for 'in your head' (*mentally calculated*)
**Comparison:** not gradable
**Modifies:** adjectives and participles, whole clauses, verbs
**Form:** -ly from *mental*; double *l*
**Contrast:** Russian *морально готов* and *морально устал* are *mentally ready* and *mentally exhausted*, not *morally*. Avoid *mentally retarded*: it is now offensive; American English says *intellectual disability*.

Mentally has 2 distinct senses.

**1. In terms of the mind or mental health.** (психически, умственно, морально)

1. Marathon runners have to be *mentally* tough.
2. He was *mentally* ill for years before anyone noticed.
3. *Mentally*, I'm ready for the exam; I just need some sleep.

**2. In your mind, without speaking, writing, or doing anything.** (мысленно, в уме)

4. I *mentally* added up the bill while the waiter talked.
5. She *mentally* went over her speech one more time.

### officially

**Pronunciation:** /əˈfɪʃəli/ &middot; *uh-FISH-uh-lee*
**Русский:** официально
**Type:** viewpoint; also manner and stance
**Position:** any position — mid before the verb (*officially opens*); front with a comma for the public version (*Officially, …*)
**Comparison:** not gradable
**Modifies:** verbs, whole clauses, adjectives and participles
**Form:** -ly from *official*; double *l*
**Contrast:** Russian *официально* also means formal in style — *официально одет* is *formally dressed*, *официальный тон* is *a formal tone*. English *officially* only means by authority or for the record. Sense 3 (*It's officially summer*) is informal but very common in American speech.

Officially has 3 distinct senses.

**1. By people in authority or according to the rules; for the record.** (официально)

1. The new bridge *officially* opens on Monday.
2. The White House has not *officially* confirmed the report.
3. You aren't *officially* enrolled until you pay the fee.

**2. According to what is said publicly, which may not be the whole truth.** (официально, по официальной версии)

4. *Officially*, he resigned; everyone knows he was fired.
5. *Officially*, the trip was about trade, but the senator spent most of it on the beach.

**3. Informal: now definitely, beyond any doubt.** (окончательно, теперь уже точно)

6. It's ninety degrees — summer is *officially* here.
7. I'm *officially* addicted to this show.

### ideally
