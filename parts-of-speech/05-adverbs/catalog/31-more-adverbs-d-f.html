More adverbs: D–F#

Adverbs D–F beyond the grammar groups — what a check against WordNet’s adverb lemmas showed a learner still needs.

← The grammar of adverbs · All groups

Mostly -ly adverbs of manner, plus the multi-word adverbs (by and large, on purpose). WordNet lists far more than this. The rest were archaic, technical, British-only, or -ly forms nobody uses, and a rejection pass left them out on purpose.

Day after day stresses tiring repetition; day by day stresses gradual change. The day after tomorrow (послезавтра) is unrelated.

Contrast
Much more often an adjective before a noun: the de facto leader, a de facto ban. Its legal opposite is de jure (по закону).
