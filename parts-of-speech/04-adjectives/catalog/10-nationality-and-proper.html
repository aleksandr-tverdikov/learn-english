Nationalities and other proper adjectives#

Russian, American, Catholic, Victorian — the adjectives English always capitalizes.

← The grammar of adjectives · All groups

In English, adjectives of nationality, religion and origin are always capitalized — Russian, American, Catholic — where Russian writes them in lowercase. Many of them also work as nouns for a person (a Russian) or a language (Russian), and a few have a separate noun altogether (Polish, but a Pole; Dutch, but a Dutchman). These entries cover the adjective and flag the nouns that differ.

American#

Pronunciation
/əˈmɛrɪkən/ · uh-MAIR-i-kuhn
Русский
американский
Type
origin
Position
both — an American company, The company is American
Comparison
not gradable — but very American, more American when it means 'typical of the US'
Form
-an from America
Contrast
In American usage American means 'of the United States'. For the continents English says North American, South American or Latin American, and Canadians and Mexicans do not call themselves American. She's American (adjective) and She's an American (noun) both work, and the adjective is more usual. Ancestry compounds take no hyphen in current American style: Mexican American, Italian American, African American. Always a capital A, unlike Russian американский.

American has 2 distinct senses.

1. Of or from the United States, its people or its culture. (американский (о США))

  1. My grandfather became an American citizen in 1962.
  2. Is your car American or Japanese?
  3. Tipping 20 percent is a very American habit.

2. Of North, Central and South America as a whole. (американский (об обеих Америках))

  1. The Organization of American States includes both Canada and Brazil.
  2. Corn, potatoes and tomatoes were first grown by the native peoples of the American continents.

British#

Pronunciation
/ˈbrɪtɪʃ/ · BRIT-ish
Русский
британский
Type
origin
Position
both — a British passport, Her accent is British
Comparison
not gradable — but very British when it means 'typical of Britain'
Contrast
British covers the whole United Kingdom: England, Scotland, Wales and Northern Ireland. English is England only, so a Scot is British but not English. The person is not a British but a British person, a Briton (formal, in the news) or informally a Brit. The British (the people) takes a plural verb: The British drink a lot of tea. Americans often say English when they mean British, and Scots and Welsh people notice.

British has 2 distinct senses.

1. Of or from the United Kingdom, its people or its government. (британский)

  1. She has dual British and American citizenship.
  2. The British government announced new visa rules on Monday.
  3. Waiting patiently in line is supposed to be very British.

2. Of the English used in the United Kingdom, as opposed to American English. (британский (о варианте английского языка))

  1. In British English, a truck is a lorry.
  2. His spelling is British because he went to school in London.

Indian#

Pronunciation
/ˈɪndiən/ · IN-dee-uhn
Русский

Type
origin
Position

Comparison

Contrast
Indian has two unrelated meanings, индийский and индейский, and only context tells them apart. For the native peoples of the Americas, Native American is the usual neutral term. American Indian is still used in law, in names (the National Museum of the American Indian) and by many Native people themselves, but Indian alone in this sense can sound dated. For Americans whose families came from India, say Indian American. Indian is not Hindu: India also has large Muslim, Christian and Sikh communities. Indian summer (warm weather in the fall) is бабье лето.

Indian has 2 distinct senses.

1. Of or from India, its people or its culture. (индийский)

  1. We ordered Indian food — chicken tikka and naan.
  2. Her parents are Indian, but she was born in New Jersey.
  3. The Indian economy is growing fast.

2. Of the native peoples of North or South America. (индейский)

  1. The museum has a large collection of American Indian art.
  2. Many towns in Oklahoma were built on former Indian land.
  3. The area is part of an Indian reservation.
