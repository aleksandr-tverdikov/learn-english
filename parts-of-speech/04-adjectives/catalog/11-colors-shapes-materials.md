# Colors, shapes and materials

> *gray*, *navy*, *oval*, *wooden* — the adjectives that sit last before the noun.

[← The grammar of adjectives](../README.md) &middot; [All groups](README.md)

Colors, shapes and materials sit near the end of the adjective order, just before the noun: *a big round wooden table*. Colors compare, and some shapes only loosely (*rounder* is fine; *more square* is common). For materials, English often uses the noun itself (*a cotton shirt*, *a glass door*) rather than an adjective; the entries here are the true adjectives, such as *wooden* and *woolen*, and they note where the noun is the normal choice.

---
### black

**Pronunciation:** /blæk/ &middot; *BLAK*
**Русский:** чёрный; чернокожий; мрачный
**Type:** color; also classifying (people)
**Position:** both — *a black car*, *The car is black*
**Comparison:** -er/-est — *blacker*, *blackest*
**Opposite:** *white*
**Contrast:** For people, American newspapers now capitalize *Black*: *Black voters*. Use it as an adjective, never as a noun: *a Black man*, not *a black*, which sounds offensive; *Negro* is outdated and offensive too, so Russian *негр* has no neutral English equivalent. *A black eye* is a bruised eye (синяк под глазом), and *black and blue* means covered in bruises. For humor, *dark* is now more common than *black*: *dark humor*.

Black has 7 distinct senses.

**1. Of the darkest color, like coal or the night sky.** (чёрный)

1. She wore a long *black* dress to the gala.
2. My car is *black*, so it shows every speck of dust.
3. Her hair looked even *blacker* under the stage lights.

**2. Having little or no light; completely dark.** (тёмный, кромешный)

4. It was pitch *black* in the basement.
5. The windows were *black* — nobody was home.
6. The *blackest* skies are in the desert, far from city lights.

**3. Belonging to a group of people with dark skin, especially of African descent; usually written *Black*.** (чернокожий)

7. She was the first *Black* woman elected to the city council.
8. The museum tells the story of *Black* families in Chicago.

**4. Of coffee or tea: served without milk or cream.** (чёрный (без молока и сливок))

9. I take my coffee *black*, no sugar.
10. A *black* coffee and a blueberry muffin, please.

**5. Angry, gloomy or without hope; full of bad feeling.** (мрачный, злой, безнадёжный)

11. He's been in a *black* mood all morning.
12. She gave me a *black* look when I mentioned her ex.
13. After the plant closed, the town's future looked *black*.

**6. Evil or harmful; connected with the devil.** (чёрный, злой)

14. The villagers believed she practiced *black* magic.
15. The villain is a man with a *black* heart.

**7. Of humor or comedy: finding something funny in death, suffering or disaster.** (чёрный (о юморе))

16. The show is a *black* comedy about a family funeral home.
17. The play mixes *black* humor with real grief.

### white

**Pronunciation:** /waɪt/ &middot; *WYT*
**Русский:** белый; бледный; седой
**Type:** color; also classifying (people)
**Position:** both — *a white shirt*, *The shirt is white*; *a white lie* only before a noun
**Comparison:** -er/-est — *whiter*, *whitest*
**Opposite:** *black*
**Contrast:** *White hair* is седые волосы, and *go white* means turn pale from shock. *A white lie* is a small, polite lie (невинная ложь), not a serious one. Americans say *coffee with cream* or *coffee with milk*; *white coffee* is British. For people, American style usually writes *white* in lowercase.

White has 7 distinct senses.

**1. Of the color of snow or milk.** (белый)

1. We painted the kitchen *white* to make it look bigger.
2. He was wearing a *white* T-shirt and jeans.
3. This toothpaste promises *whiter* teeth in two weeks.

**2. Belonging to a group of people with light skin, especially of European descent.** (белый (о расе))

4. Most of the town's residents are *white*.
5. The survey asked *white* voters and Black voters the same questions.

**3. Pale in the face from fear, shock or illness.** (бледный, побелевший)

6. He went *white* when he saw the bill.
7. She was *white* as a sheet after the accident.

**4. Of hair: completely gray from age.** (седой)

8. My grandfather's hair turned *white* in his sixties.
9. A woman with short *white* hair answered the door.

**5. Of wine, bread or meat: lighter in color than other kinds.** (белый, светлый)

10. Would you like red or *white* wine with the fish?
11. Chicken breast is *white* meat.
12. The kids will only eat *white* bread.

**6. Harmless and told to be kind or polite, in *a white lie*.** (невинный (о лжи))

13. I told a *white* lie and said I loved the sweater.
14. Everyone tells a *white* lie now and then.

**7. Of a holiday or season: with snow on the ground.** (снежный)

15. Denver hasn't had a *white* Christmas in years.
16. The kids were hoping for a *white* Christmas.

### gray

**Pronunciation:** /ɡreɪ/ &middot; *GRAY*
**Русский:** серый; седой; пасмурный
**Type:** color
**Position:** both — *a gray sky*, *The sky is gray*
**Comparison:** -er/-est — *grayer*, *grayest*
**Contrast:** American spelling is *gray*; *grey* is British, though it survives in names (*Greyhound*, *Earl Grey*). *Gray hair* is седые волосы, not серые, and *go gray* means поседеть. *A gray area* is a situation where the rules are unclear, not a place.

Gray has 6 distinct senses.

**1. Of the color between black and white, like ash or rain clouds.** (серый)

1. He wore a *gray* suit to the interview.
2. The paint dried *grayer* than it looked in the can.
3. Her eyes are *gray*, not blue.

**2. Of hair: turning white with age; of a person: having such hair.** (седой)

4. My dad started going *gray* at forty.
5. She has a few *gray* hairs and doesn't care.

**3. Of weather or the sky: cloudy, dull and without sun.** (пасмурный, хмурый)

6. It was a cold, *gray* morning in Seattle.
7. The sky has been *gray* all week.

**4. Of the face or skin: pale and unhealthy from illness, tiredness or shock.** (серый, землистый, бледный)

8. He looked *gray* and exhausted after the night shift.
9. Her face went *gray* when the doctor walked in.

**5. Dull and without interest or personality.** (серый, скучный, безликий)

10. Life in the suburbs felt *gray* after New York.
11. The hero is a *gray* little clerk who never takes a risk.

**6. Not clearly right or wrong, or not clearly defined.** (неопределённый, спорный)

12. Cash tips are a *gray* area for a lot of workers.
13. The law is pretty *gray* on this point.

### brown

**Pronunciation:** /braʊn/ &middot; *BROWN*
**Русский:** коричневый; карий; смуглый; загорелый
**Type:** color
**Position:** both — *brown shoes*, *The shoes are brown*
**Comparison:** -er/-est — *browner*, *brownest*
**Contrast:** *Brown eyes* are карие глаза — English has no separate word. *Brown hair* is каштановые or тёмно-русые волосы; Russian *русые* is usually *light brown* or *dark blond*. *Brown rice* and *brown sugar* are about how the food is processed, not only about the color. About people, *brown* is an adjective, mostly in phrases like *Black and brown communities*; never use it as a noun for a person.

Brown has 5 distinct senses.

**1. Of the color of wood, soil or coffee.** (коричневый)

1. He bought a *brown* leather jacket.
2. The lawn turns *brown* every August.
3. The leaves get *browner* as fall goes on.

**2. Of eyes: dark; of hair: any shade from light to dark brown.** (карий (о глазах); каштановый, тёмно-русый (о волосах))

4. The baby has big *brown* eyes.
5. His hair is light *brown* with a little gray.

**3. Belonging to a group of people with light to moderately dark skin, especially Latino, Middle Eastern or South Asian; sometimes written *Brown*.** (смуглый, темнокожий (о латиноамериканцах, выходцах из Южной Азии и др.))

6. The program mostly serves Black and *brown* students.
7. Advocates say *brown* and Black neighborhoods get fewer parks.

**4. Of skin: darkened by the sun; tanned.** (загорелый, смуглый)

8. The kids came back from camp *brown* from the sun.
9. By August he was as *brown* as a lifeguard.

**5. Of rice, bread or sugar: not fully refined, keeping more of the grain or the dark syrup.** (бурый (о рисе), из муки грубого помола; коричневый (о сахаре))

10. I'll have the chicken with *brown* rice.
11. Add a cup of packed *brown* sugar.

### pink

**Pronunciation:** /pɪŋk/ &middot; *PINK*
**Русский:** розовый; раскрасневшийся
**Type:** color
**Position:** both — *a pink dress*, *The dress is pink*
**Comparison:** -er/-est — *pinker*, *pinkest*
**Contrast:** *Tickled pink* means very pleased, not ticklish. *A pink slip* (a noun) is a notice that you have lost your job. For a face red from embarrassment, *pink* is milder than *red*: *She went pink* is a light blush.

Pink has 2 distinct senses.

**1. Of a pale red color.** (розовый)

1. She painted her nails bright *pink*.
2. My daughter wanted a *pink* bike for her birthday.
3. The sky got *pinker* as the sun went down.

**2. Of the face or skin: slightly red from health, heat or embarrassment.** (розовый, румяный, раскрасневшийся)

4. His cheeks went *pink* when she thanked him.
5. The baby's skin was soft and *pink*.

### purple

**Pronunciation:** /ˈpɜːrpəl/ &middot; *PUR-puhl*
**Русский:** фиолетовый, лиловый; багровый
**Type:** color
**Position:** both — *a purple scarf*, *The scarf is purple*; *purple prose* only before a noun
**Comparison:** -er/-est or more/most — *purpler*, *purplest*, *more purple*
**Contrast:** False friend: Russian *пурпурный* leans toward crimson, while *purple* is фиолетовый or лиловый — the color of eggplant and grape juice. *Violet* is a bluer purple; *lavender* and *lilac* are pale purples. In US politics *red* is Republican and *blue* is Democratic, so a *purple* state is split between them.

Purple has 4 distinct senses.

**1. Of a color between red and blue.** (фиолетовый, лиловый)

1. The grapes were dark *purple* and very sweet.
2. She dyed her hair *purple* for the concert.
3. The bruise turned *more purple* overnight.

**2. Of the face or skin: dark red from anger, cold or effort.** (багровый)

4. He turned *purple* with rage.
5. Her hands were *purple* from the cold.

**3. Of writing: too elaborate, full of fancy words and emotion.** (напыщенный, цветистый)

6. The novel is full of *purple* prose about sunsets.
7. Cut the *purple* passages and get to the point.

**4. Of a US state or area: with about equal support for Republicans and Democrats.** (колеблющийся (о штате, округе))

8. Colorado used to be a *purple* state.
9. The suburbs around Atlanta are turning *purple*.

### orange

**Pronunciation:** /ˈɔːrɪndʒ/ &middot; *OR-inj*
**Русский:** оранжевый; рыжий
**Type:** color
**Position:** both — *an orange vest*, *The vest is orange*
**Comparison:** more/most — *more orange*, *most orange*
**Contrast:** Red human hair is *red* in English, not *orange*: a рыжий человек is *a redhead*. For animals, *orange* is normal: *an orange cat*. In *orange juice* the modifier is the noun *orange* (the fruit), not the color adjective.

Orange has 2 distinct senses.

**1. Of a color between red and yellow.** (оранжевый)

1. Hunters wear *orange* vests in the woods.
2. The maple leaves turn *orange* in October.
3. The sunset got *more orange* as we watched.

**2. Of an animal's fur: reddish yellow.** (рыжий)

4. We adopted an *orange* tabby from the shelter.
5. Our dog is white with *orange* patches on her back.

### yellow

**Pronunciation:** /ˈjɛloʊ/ &middot; *YEL-oh*
**Русский:** жёлтый; трусливый
**Type:** color
**Position:** both — *a yellow bus*, *The bus is yellow*
**Comparison:** -er/-est — *yellower*, *yellowest*
**Contrast:** Russian *жёлтая пресса* is *the tabloids* or *tabloid journalism* in modern American English; *yellow journalism* is mainly a historical term for the 1890s newspaper wars. *Yellow* meaning cowardly is informal and old-fashioned. The middle traffic light is *yellow* in American English (*run a yellow light*); *amber* is British. Never use *yellow* to describe people of East Asian descent: it is a racial slur.

Yellow has 3 distinct senses.

**1. Of the color of lemons or butter.** (жёлтый)

1. The kids ran out to the *yellow* school bus.
2. Our house is *yellow* with white trim.
3. The old photos have gotten *yellower* with age.

**2. Cowardly — informal and old-fashioned.** (трусливый)

4. He was too *yellow* to stand up to his boss.
5. Don't be *yellow* — just go talk to her.

**3. Of news reporting: shocking and exaggerated to sell papers, in *yellow journalism*.** (жёлтый, бульварный (о прессе))

6. Hearst's papers were famous for *yellow* journalism.
7. Critics called the cable coverage a new kind of *yellow* journalism.

### green

**Pronunciation:** /ɡriːn/ &middot; *GREEN*
**Русский:** зелёный; незрелый; неопытный; экологичный
**Type:** color; also quality
**Position:** both — *green eyes*, *Her eyes are green*
**Comparison:** -er/-est — *greener*, *greenest*
**Takes:** *with envy* — only in the 'jealous' sense: *green with envy*
**Contrast:** *Green* for 'inexperienced' matches Russian *зелёный*. Envy is *green with envy*, not *green from envy*. *Green* in *green energy* or *go green* means good for the environment, not the color. *A green card* is the US permanent resident card, and *greens* (a noun) are leafy vegetables.

Green has 8 distinct senses.

**1. Of the color of grass and leaves.** (зелёный)

1. She has bright *green* eyes.
2. Wait until the light turns *green*.
3. The grass is always *greener* on the other side.

**2. Covered with grass, trees or other plants.** (зелёный, озеленённый)

4. Portland has a lot of *green* space for a big city.
5. The hills are *green* after the spring rains.

**3. Of fruit: not yet ripe.** (незрелый, зелёный)

6. These bananas are still *green*.
7. Don't pick the tomatoes while they're *green*.

**4. Young and without experience.** (неопытный, зелёный)

8. The new hires are pretty *green*, so be patient with them.
9. I was *green* when I started, and I made every mistake.

**5. Good for the environment, or supporting its protection.** (экологичный, зелёный)

10. The company is trying to go *green*.
11. *Green* energy now powers half the city.
12. Stores are switching to *greener* packaging.

**6. Pale and sick-looking, as if about to vomit.** (позеленевший, бледный (от тошноты))

13. He looked a little *green* after the roller coaster.
14. She turned *green* as soon as the boat left the dock.

**7. Very jealous, in *green with envy*.** (завидующий)

15. My friends were *green* with envy when they saw my new car.
16. His success left the other writers *green* with envy.

**8. Of wood: freshly cut and not yet dried.** (сырой (о древесине))

17. *Green* wood smokes a lot when you burn it.
18. Don't build the deck with *green* lumber.

### blue

**Pronunciation:** /bluː/ &middot; *BLOO*
**Русский:** синий, голубой; грустный
**Type:** color; also emotion
**Position:** both — *blue eyes*, *Her eyes are blue*; 'sad' mostly after a verb: *feeling blue*
**Comparison:** -er/-est — *bluer*, *bluest*
**Contrast:** English has one basic word, *blue*, for both синий and голубой: say *light blue* for голубой and *dark blue* or *navy* for тёмно-синий. Russian slang *голубой* ('gay') does not carry over — *blue* never means gay. In US politics *blue* is Democratic and *red* is Republican, the reverse of most European party colors. Spelling: *bluer*, *bluest*, with no extra *e*.

Blue has 5 distinct senses.

**1. Of the color of a clear sky or the sea.** (синий, голубой)

1. She has bright *blue* eyes.
2. The sky was *blue* all weekend.
3. The water gets *bluer* the farther out you go.

**2. Sad or depressed — informal.** (грустный, подавленный)

4. I've been feeling kind of *blue* since the holidays.
5. Rainy Sundays always make me *blue*.

**3. In US politics: voting for or controlled by Democrats.** (демократический (о штате, округе))

6. California is a solidly *blue* state.
7. The district turned *blue* in the last election.

**4. Of the skin or lips: bluish from cold or lack of air.** (посиневший)

8. The kids' lips were *blue* after swimming in the lake.
9. His fingers were *blue* with cold.

**5. Of jokes or language: rude, especially about sex.** (неприличный, непристойный)

10. The comedian's act was a little too *blue* for TV.
11. He told some *blue* jokes at the bachelor party.

### beige

**Pronunciation:** /beɪʒ/ &middot; *BAYZH*
**Русский:** бежевый
**Type:** color
**Position:** both — *a beige sofa*, *The sofa is beige*
**Comparison:** more/most — *more beige*, *most beige*
**Contrast:** The word ends in the soft *zh* sound of *garage*, not the *j* sound of *badge*. Americans also say *tan* for a darker beige and *khaki* for the light brownish color of casual pants. *Beige* meaning 'boring' is informal.

Beige has 2 distinct senses.

**1. Of a pale sandy brown color.** (бежевый)

1. We picked a *beige* sofa that goes with everything.
2. The walls in the rental are all *beige*.
3. The new carpet is even *more beige* than the old one.

**2. Boring and without character — informal.** (скучный, безликий)

4. The hotel was clean but completely *beige*.
5. Their music is pleasant but a little *beige*.

**Type:** color
**Position:** both — *a navy blazer*, *The blazer is navy*
**Comparison:** not gradable
**Contrast:** *Navy* alone as a color means *navy blue*. Before nouns like *officer* or *base*, *Navy* is the noun (*a Navy officer*); the adjective for 'relating to a navy' is *naval* (военно-морской): *a naval base*.

Of a very dark blue color.

1. He wore a *navy* blazer with khakis.
2. The team's uniforms are *navy* with gold numbers.

**Русский:** тёмно-бордовый
**Type:** color
**Position:** both — *a maroon sweater*, *The sweater is maroon*
**Comparison:** not gradable

Of a dark brownish-red color.

### turquoise

**Type:** color
**Position:** both — *turquoise water*, *The water is turquoise*
**Comparison:** more/most — *more turquoise*, *most turquoise*

Of a bright greenish-blue color.

### golden

**Pronunciation:** /ˈɡoʊldən/ &middot; *GOHL-duhn*
**Русский:** золотистый; золотой
**Type:** color; also evaluative
**Position:** both — the color and *you're golden* after a verb; *a golden opportunity*, *golden age*, *golden anniversary* only before a noun
**Comparison:** more/most — *more golden*, *most golden*
**Form:** -en from *gold*
**Contrast:** *Gold* (the noun) is the usual word for things made of gold: *a gold ring*, *gold earrings*. *Golden* is mostly the color (*golden hair*, *golden brown*) and the figurative senses (*a golden opportunity*); *a golden ring* sounds like a fairy tale. *You're golden* is informal American English for 'you're all set'.

Golden has 7 distinct senses.

**1. Having the color of gold; shiny yellow.** (золотистый)

1. Bake the pie until the crust is *golden* brown.
2. She has long *golden* hair.
3. The fields looked *more golden* in the evening light.

**2. Made of gold — literary or old-fashioned.** (золотой)

4. The king wore a *golden* crown.
5. In the story, the goose lays *golden* eggs.

**3. Of a period: very happy, successful or prosperous.** (золотой (о времени))

6. The 1930s and '40s were the *golden* age of Hollywood.
7. They spent their *golden* years traveling the country.

**4. Very good and likely to bring success, of a chance or opportunity.** (прекрасный, уникальный (о возможности))

8. This is a *golden* opportunity to meet investors.
9. He missed a *golden* chance to tie the game.

**5. Admired by everyone and expected to succeed, in *golden boy* and *golden girl*.** (всеобщий любимец, баловень судьбы)

10. He was the *golden* boy of Silicon Valley.
11. In high school she was the *golden* girl who could do no wrong.

**6. Of an anniversary: the fiftieth.** (золотой (о юбилее))

12. My parents celebrated their *golden* anniversary in June.
13. A *golden* anniversary calls for a big party.

**7. In a good position, with nothing to worry about — informal, after a verb.** (всё в порядке, в шоколаде)

14. Get a B on the final and you're *golden*.
15. Once the paperwork is signed, we're *golden*.

### silver

**Pronunciation:** /ˈsɪlvər/ &middot; *SIL-vur*
**Русский:** серебристый; седой; серебряный (о юбилее)
**Type:** color
**Position:** both — *a silver car*, *The car is silver*; the anniversary sense only before a noun
**Comparison:** not gradable — *silvery* is the word for 'somewhat silver'
**Contrast:** *A silver ring* (серебряное кольцо) uses the noun *silver* for the material, like *a gold ring*, so 'made of silver' is not a sense here. The adjective is the color: *a silver car* is серебристая машина. *Silvery* means 'somewhat silver' and also describes a soft, clear sound: *a silvery voice*. *Silver hair* is a flattering way to say gray hair.

Silver has 3 distinct senses.

**1. Having the shiny gray color of silver.** (серебристый)

1. She drives a *silver* Honda.
2. Her dress was *silver* and covered in sequins.
3. The *silver* balloons matched the tablecloths.

**2. Of hair: gray or white in a shiny, attractive way.** (седой, серебристый)

4. The anchor was a handsome man with *silver* hair.
5. Her hair went *silver* in her fifties, and she loved it.

**3. Of an anniversary: the twenty-fifth.** (серебряный (о юбилее))

6. They threw a party for their *silver* anniversary.
7. The theater celebrates its *silver* jubilee this year.

4. Their son is *blond* like his mother.

**3. Of wood, beer and some other things: light in color.** (светлый)

6. The table is made of *blond* oak.
7. The brewery's *blond* ale is light and crisp.

### pale

**Pronunciation:** /peɪl/ &middot; *PAYL*
**Русский:** бледный; светлый
**Type:** color; also quality
**Position:** both — *a pale face*, *You look pale*; *a pale imitation* only before a noun
**Comparison:** -er/-est — *paler*, *palest*
